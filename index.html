01

Music is the arrangement of sound to create some combination of form, harmony, melody, rhythm, or otherwise expressive content.

音乐是对声音进行编排，然后创造出来一些组合形式，这些组合是和声、旋律、节奏或者其他有表现力内容的组合。

Music is generally agreed to be a cultural universal that is present in all human societies.

人们普遍认为，音乐是一种共通的文化形式，存在于所有人类社会当中。

Definitions of music vary widely in substance and approach.

关于音乐的定义，内容和解读角度各不相同。

While scholars agree that music is defined by a small number of specific elements, there is no consensus as to what these necessary elements are.

学者们都认同，音乐由少数特定要素构成。但对于这些必备要素具体是什么，大家并没有统一看法。

Music is often characterized as a highly versatile medium for expressing human creativity.

人们常这样形容音乐，它是一种用途极广的载体，能够展现人类的创造力。

Diverse activities are involved in the creation of music, and are often divided into categories of composition, improvisation, and performance.

音乐创作包含多种活动。这些活动通常分为三类，分别是作曲、即兴演奏和表演。

Music may be performed using a wide variety of musical instruments, including the human voice.

演奏音乐可以使用各式各样的乐器，人声也包含在内。

It can also be composed, sequenced, or otherwise produced to be indirectly played mechanically or electronically, such as via a music box, barrel organ, or digital audio workstation software on a computer.

音乐也可以经过编曲、编排或是其他方式制作。它能借助机械、电子设备间接播放，比如八音盒、手摇风琴，还有电脑上的数字音频工作站软件。

02

Music often plays a key role in social events and religious ceremonies.

音乐常常发挥重要作用，常见于各类社交活动与宗教仪式之中。

The techniques of making music are often transmitted as part of a cultural tradition.

音乐的创作技艺，往往会代代相传，成为文化传统的一部分。

Music is played in public and private contexts, highlighted at events such as festivals and concerts for various different types of ensembles.

音乐可在公开或私下场合演奏。节庆、音乐会等活动中也会重点呈现，参演的演奏团体类型各不相同。

Music is used in the production of other media, such as in soundtracks to films, TV shows, operas, and video games.

音乐也应用于其他媒介创作，比如电影、电视剧、歌剧和电子游戏的配乐。

03

Listening to music is a common means of entertainment.

欣赏音乐是一种常见的娱乐方式。

The culture surrounding music extends into areas of academic study, journalism, philosophy, psychology, and therapy.

与音乐相关的文化还延伸至学术研究、新闻、哲学、心理学及理疗等领域。

The music industry includes songwriters, performers, sound engineers, producers, tour organizers, distributors of instruments, accessories, and publishers of sheet music and recordings.

音乐行业包含词曲作者、表演者、音响工程师、制作人、巡演策划、乐器及配件经销商，还有乐谱与音像制品出版商。

Technology facilitating the recording and reproduction of music has historically included sheet music, microphones, phonographs, and tape machines, with playback of digital music being a common use for MP3 players, CD players, and smartphones.

历史上，助力音乐录制与播放的技术载体包括乐谱、麦克风、留声机和磁带机；如今MP3播放器、CD机和智能手机则普遍用于播放数字音乐。

Etymology and terminology

04

The modern English word music came into use in the 1630s.

It descends from Middle English musike, which in turn descends from Old French musique, then Latin mūsica—and ultimately from Ancient Greek mousiké technē (μουσική τέχνη), a phrase meaning “art of the Muses”. The Muses were nine deities in Ancient Greek mythology who presided over the arts and sciences. They were included in tales by the earliest Western authors, Homer and Hesiod, and eventually came to be associated with music specifically. Over time, Polyhymnia would reside over music more prominently than the other muses. The Latin word musica was also the originator for both the Spanish música and French musique via spelling and linguistic adjustment, though other European terms were probably loanwords, including the Italian musica, German Musik, Dutch muziek, Norwegian musikk, Polish muzyka, and Russian muzïka.

现代英语单词 music 于 1630年代开始使用。

它源自中古英语 musike，中古英语又源自古法语 musique，再源自拉丁语 mūsica——最终可追溯至古希腊语 mousiké technē（μουσική τέχνη），意为”缪斯的艺术”。缪斯是古希腊神话中掌管文艺与科学的九位女神。她们最早出现在西方最古老的作家——荷马与赫西俄德的作品中，后来逐渐被专门与音乐联系在一起。随着时间推移，波吕许谟尼亚（Polyhymnia）在众缪斯中逐渐成为音乐的主要守护者。拉丁语 musica 也是西班牙语 música 和法语 musique 的词源，经由拼写和语言调整演变而来；而其他欧洲语言中的对应词大概率是借词，包括意大利语 musica、德语 Musik、荷兰语 muziek、挪威语 musikk、波兰语 muzyka 和俄语 muzïka。

04

The modern Western world usually defines music as an all-encompassing term used to describe diverse genres, styles, and traditions. This is not the case worldwide, and languages such as modern Indonesian (musik) and Shona (musakazo) have recently adopted words to reflect this universal conception, as they did not have words that fit exactly the Western scope. Before Western contact in East Asia, neither Japan nor China had a single word that encompasses music in a broad sense, but culturally, they often regarded music in such a fashion. The closest word to mean music in Chinese, yue, shares a character with le, meaning joy, and originally referred to all the arts before narrowing in meaning. Africa is too diverse to make firm generalizations, but the musicologist J. H. Kwabena Nketia has emphasized African music’s often inseparable connection to dance and speech in general. Some African cultures, such as the Songye people of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and the Tiv people of Nigeria, have a strong and broad conception of ‘music’ but no corresponding word in their native languages. Other words commonly translated as music often have more specific meanings in their respective cultures: the Hindi word for music, sangita, properly refers to art music, while the many Indigenous languages of the Americas have words for music that refer specifically to song but describe instrumental music regardless. Though the Arabic musiqi can refer to all music, it is usually used for instrumental and metric music, while khandan identifies vocal and improvised music.

现代西方世界通常将音乐定义为一个包罗万象的术语，用来描述各种流派、风格和传统。但全球并非如此。例如现代印尼语（musik）和绍纳语（musakazo）等语言近期才采纳了词语来反映这一普遍概念，因为它们原本没有恰好对应西方范畴的词汇。在东亚与西方接触之前，日本和中国都没有一个能在广义上涵盖音乐的单一词语，但在文化上，它们常以这种方式看待音乐。中文中与”音乐”最接近的字是”乐”（yuè），它与表示快乐的”乐”（lè）共用一个字，最初指所有艺术，后来词义才收窄。非洲过于多元，难以做出确定的概括，但音乐学家J. H. Kwabena Nketia强调，非洲音乐与舞蹈及言语之间往往存在不可分割的联系。一些非洲文化，如刚果民主共和国的松耶人和尼日利亚的蒂夫人，对”音乐”有强烈而宽泛的概念，但其母语中并无对应的词语。其他常被译为”音乐”的词语在各自文化中往往有更具体的含义：印地语中表示音乐的词sangīta，严格来说指的是艺术音乐；而美洲的众多原住民语言中，表示音乐的词语专门指歌曲，却可以泛指器乐。阿拉伯语musīqī虽可指所有音乐，但通常用于器乐和有节拍的音乐，而khandan则特指声乐和即兴音乐。

History

Origins and prehistory

04

It is often debated to what extent the origins of music will ever be understood, and there are competing theories that aim to explain it. Many scholars highlight a relationship between the origin of music and the origin of language, and there is disagreement surrounding whether music developed before, after, or simultaneously with language. A similar source of contention surrounds whether music was the intentional result of natural selection or was a byproduct spandrel of evolution. The earliest influential theory was proposed by Charles Darwin in 1871, who stated that music arose as a form of sexual selection, perhaps via mating calls. Darwin’s original perspective has been heavily criticized for its inconsistencies with other sexual selection methods, though many scholars in the 21st century have developed and promoted the theory. Other theories include that music arose to assist in organizing labor, improving long-distance communication, benefiting communication with the divine, assisting in community cohesion, or as a defense to scare off predators.

音乐的起源究竟能在多大程度上被理解，这一问题历来争议不断，学界也存在多种相互竞争的理论试图对此作出解释。许多学者强调音乐起源与语言起源之间的关联，但对于音乐是早于、晚于还是与语言同时发展，意见并不统一。另一个争论焦点在于：音乐是自然选择的有意产物，还是进化过程中的副产物（即”拱肩”效应）。最早产生重大影响的理论由查尔斯·达尔文于1871年提出，他认为音乐源于性选择，可能通过求偶叫声而产生。达尔文的原始观点因与其他性选择机制存在矛盾而受到严厉批评，不过21世纪仍有不少学者对该理论加以发展和推广。其他理论包括：音乐的产生是为了协助组织劳动、改善远距离通讯、便利与神灵的沟通、促进社群凝聚，或作为吓退捕食者的防御手段。

04

Prehistoric music can only be theorized based on findings from paleolithic archaeology sites. The disputed Divje Babe flute, a perforated cave bear femur, is at least 40,000 years old, though there is considerable debate surrounding whether it is truly a musical instrument or an object formed by animals. The earliest objects whose designations as musical instruments are widely accepted are bone flutes from the Swabian Jura, Germany, namely from the Geissenklösterle, Hohle Fels, and Vogelherd caves. Dated to the Aurignacian (of the Upper Paleolithic) and used by Early European modern humans, from all three caves there are eight examples, four made from the wing bones of birds and four from mammoth ivory; three of these are near complete. Three flutes from the Geissenklösterle are dated as the oldest, c. 43,150–39,370 BP.

史前音乐只能基于旧石器时代考古遗址的发现来加以推测。存在争议的迪夫耶巴贝长笛——一根带孔的洞熊股骨——至少有40,000年历史，但关于它究竟是真正的乐器还是动物造成的物件，争议颇大。目前被广泛认可为乐器的最早物件，是德国施瓦本汝拉山出土的骨笛，具体来自盖森克洛斯特莱、霍勒费尔斯和福格尔赫德三处洞穴。这些骨笛可追溯至奥瑞纳文化期（旧石器时代晚期），由早期欧洲现代人使用，三处洞穴中共出土八件，其中四件由鸟翼骨制成，四件由猛犸象牙制成，三件接近完整。盖森克洛斯特莱出土的三支长笛年代最早，约为43,150至39,370年前。

Antiquity

04

The earliest material and representational evidence of Egyptian musical instruments dates to the Predynastic period, but the evidence is more securely attested in the Old Kingdom when harps, flutes, and double clarinets were played. Percussion instruments, lyres, and lutes were added to orchestras by the Middle Kingdom. Cymbals frequently accompanied music and dance, much as they still do in Egypt today. Egyptian folk music, including the traditional Sufi dhikr rituals, are the closest contemporary music genre to ancient Egyptian music, having preserved many of its features, rhythms and instruments.

埃及乐器最早的实物与图像证据可追溯至前王朝时期，但证据最为确凿的是古王国时期，当时已有竖琴、长笛和双管单簧类乐器。中王国时期，打击乐器、里拉琴和琉特琴也被加入乐队。铜钹常与音乐和舞蹈相伴，正如今天的埃及依然如此。埃及民间音乐，包括传统苏菲派齐克尔仪式，是当代最接近古埃及音乐的音乐类型，保留了其许多特征、节奏和乐器。

04

The “Hurrian Hymn to Nikkal”, found on clay tablets in the ancient Syrian city of Ugarit, is the oldest surviving notated work of music, dating back to approximately 1400 BCE.

在古叙利亚城市乌加里特的泥板上发现的”胡里安人致尼卡尔颂歌”，是现存最古老的有记谱的音乐作品，可追溯至约公元前1400年。

04

Music was an important part of social and cultural life in ancient Greece and was one of the main subjects taught to children. Musical education was considered important for the development of an individual’s soul. Musicians and singers played a prominent role in Greek theater, and those who received a musical education were seen as nobles and in perfect harmony (as can be read in the Republic, Plato). Mixed gender choruses performed for entertainment, celebration, and spiritual ceremonies. Instruments included the double-reed aulos and a plucked string instrument, the lyre, principally a special kind called a kithara. Music was an important part of education, and boys were taught music starting at age six. Greek musical literacy created significant musical development. Greek music theory included the Greek musical modes, which eventually became the basis for Western religious and classical music. Later, influences from the Roman Empire, Eastern Europe, and the Byzantine Empire changed Greek music. The Seikilos epitaph is the oldest surviving example of a complete musical composition, including musical notation, from anywhere in the world. The oldest surviving work written about music theory is Harmonika Stoicheia by Aristoxenus.

音乐是古希腊社会与文化生活的重要组成部分，也是儿童教育的主要科目之一。音乐教育被认为对个人灵魂的发展至关重要。乐师和歌手在希腊戏剧中扮演着突出角色，接受过音乐教育的人被视为贵族，且处于完美和谐之中（正如柏拉图在《理想国》中所述）。男女混合合唱团用于娱乐、庆典和宗教仪式。乐器包括双簧管类的阿夫洛斯管和拨弦乐器里拉琴，其中主要是一种名为基萨拉琴的特殊类型。音乐是教育的重要组成部分，男孩从六岁起便开始学习音乐。希腊人的音乐读写能力推动了音乐的显著发展。希腊乐理包括希腊调式，这些调式最终成为西方宗教音乐和古典音乐的基础。此后，罗马帝国、东欧和拜占庭帝国的影响改变了希腊音乐。塞基洛斯墓志铭是全世界现存最古老的完整音乐作品，包括乐谱在内。现存最古老的音乐理论著作是阿里斯多克塞努斯所著的《和谐的要素》。

Asian cultures

04

Asian music covers a swath of music cultures surveyed in the articles on Arabia, Central Asia, East Asia, South Asia, and Southeast Asia. These traditions, with several reaching into antiquity, share historical ties through trade, religion and philosophical exchange, yet preserve their unique local aesthetics and performance styles.

亚洲音乐涵盖了阿拉伯、中亚、东亚、南亚和东南亚等条目中所涉及的广泛音乐文化。这些传统中有若干可追溯至古代，它们通过贸易、宗教和哲学交流而存在历史纽带，但各自保留了独特的地方美学与表演风格。

04

Indian classical music is one of the oldest musical traditions in the world. Sculptures from the Indus Valley civilization show dance and old musical instruments, like the seven-holed flute. Stringed instruments and drums have been recovered from Harappa and Mohenjo Daro by excavations carried out by Mortimer Wheeler. The Rigveda, an ancient Hindu text, has elements of present Indian music, with musical notation to denote the meter and mode of chanting. Indian classical music (marga) is monophonic, and based on a single melody line or raga rhythmically organized through talas. The poem Cilappatikaram provides information about how new scales can be formed by modal shifting of the tonic from an existing scale. Present day Hindi music was influenced by Persian traditional music and Afghan Mughals. Carnatic music, popular in the southern states, is largely devotional; the majority of the songs are addressed to the Hindu deities. There are songs emphasizing love and other social issues.

印度古典音乐是世界上最古老的音乐传统之一。印度河流域文明的雕塑展示了舞蹈和古老乐器，如七孔笛。莫蒂默·惠勒主持的发掘工作在哈拉帕和摩亨佐-达罗出土了弦乐器和鼓。《梨俱吠陀》作为古印度教典籍，已包含当今印度音乐的元素，其中有标注吟唱节拍与调式的乐谱。印度古典音乐（mārga）为单声部音乐，以单一旋律线（即拉格）为基础，通过塔拉节拍体系加以节奏组织。《锡拉帕迪卡兰》一诗提供了如何通过在既有音阶上移动主音来形成新音阶的信息。当今印地语音乐受到波斯传统音乐和阿富汗莫卧儿人的影响。流行于南部各邦的卡纳提克音乐以宗教虔诚为主，大部分歌曲献给印度教神灵，也有强调爱情及其他社会议题的歌曲。

04

Indonesian music has been formed since the Bronze Age culture migrated to the Indonesian archipelago in the 2nd-3rd centuries BCE. Indonesian traditional music uses percussion instruments, especially kendang and gongs. Some of them developed elaborate and distinctive instruments, such as the sasando stringed instrument on the island of Rote, the Sundanese angklung, and the complex and sophisticated Javanese and Balinese gamelan orchestras. Indonesia is the home of gong chime, a general term for a set of small, high pitched pot gongs. Gongs are usually placed in order of note, with the boss up on a string held in a low wooden frame. The most popular form of Indonesian music is gamelan, an ensemble of tuned percussion instruments that include metallophones, drums, gongs, and spike fiddles along with bamboo suling (like a flute).

印尼音乐的形成可追溯至公元前2至3世纪青铜时代文化向印尼群岛迁移之时。印尼传统音乐以打击乐器为主，尤其是肯东鼓和铜锣。其中一些发展出了精致而独特的乐器，如罗地岛上的沙珊多弦乐器、巽他族的昂格隆，以及复杂精密的爪哇和巴厘甘美兰乐队。印尼是锣琴的发源地，锣琴是对一组小型高音壶锣的统称。铜锣通常按音高排列，悬挂于低矮木架上的绳索中，凸面朝上。印尼最流行的音乐形式是甘美兰，这是一种由调音打击乐器组成的合奏，包括金属琴、鼓、铜锣和尖桩 fiddle，以及竹制苏灵笛（类似长笛）。

04

Chinese classical music, the traditional art or court music of China, has a history stretching over about 3,000 years. It has its own unique systems of musical notation, as well as musical tuning and pitch, musical instruments and styles or genres. Chinese music is pentatonic-diatonic, having a scale of twelve notes to an octave (5 + 7 = 12) as does European-influenced music.

中国古典音乐，即中国的传统艺术音乐或宫廷音乐，已有约三千年的历史。它拥有独特的记谱体系、律制与音高体系、乐器以及风格或体裁。中国音乐属五声—七声音阶体系，一个八度内包含十二个音（5+7=12），与受欧洲影响的音乐相同。

Western classical

04

Early music

The medieval music era (500 to 1400), which took place during the Middle Ages, started with the introduction of monophonic (single melodic line) chanting into Catholic Church services. Musical notation was used since ancient times in Greek culture, but in the Middle Ages, notation was first introduced by the Catholic Church, so chant melodies could be written down, to facilitate the use of the same melodies for religious music across the Catholic empire. The only European Medieval repertory that has been found, in written form, from before 800 is the monophonic liturgical plainsong chant of the Catholic Church, the central tradition of which was called Gregorian chant. Alongside these traditions of sacred and church music there existed a vibrant tradition of secular song (non-religious songs). Examples of composers from this period are Léonin, Pérotin, Guillaume de Machaut, and Walther von der Vogelweide.

早期音乐

中世纪音乐时期（500至1400年）始于将单声部（单一旋律线）吟唱引入天主教礼拜仪式。记谱法在古希腊文化中早已使用，但在中世纪，记谱法首次由天主教会引入，以便将吟唱旋律记录下来，从而在整个天主教帝国范围内统一使用相同的宗教音乐旋律。目前发现的800年以前以书面形式留存的唯一欧洲中世纪曲目，是天主教会的单声部礼拜平咏圣歌，其中心传统被称为格里高利圣咏。与这些神圣及教会音乐传统并存的，还有一种充满活力的世俗歌曲（非宗教歌曲）传统。这一时期的代表性作曲家包括莱奥南、佩罗坦、纪尧姆·德·马肖和瓦尔特·冯·德·福格尔魏德。

04

Renaissance music (c. 1400 to 1600) was more focused on secular themes, such as courtly love. Around 1450, the printing press was invented, which made printed sheet music much less expensive and easier to mass-produce (prior to the invention of the press, all notated music was hand-copied). The increased availability of sheet music spread musical styles quicker and across a larger area. Musicians and singers often worked for the church, courts, and towns. Church choirs grew in size, and the church remained an important patron of music. By the middle of the 15th century, composers wrote richly polyphonic sacred music, in which different melody lines were interwoven simultaneously. Prominent composers from this era include Guillaume Du Fay, Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina, Thomas Morley, Orlando di Lasso, and Josquin des Prez. As musical activity shifted from the church to aristocratic courts, kings, queens and princes competed for the finest composers. Many leading composers came from the Netherlands, Belgium, and France; they are called the Franco-Flemish composers. They held important positions throughout Europe, especially in Italy. Other countries with vibrant musical activity included Germany, England, and Spain.

文艺复兴时期音乐（约1400至1600年）更侧重世俗主题，如宫廷爱情。约1450年，印刷机的发明使印刷乐谱的成本大幅降低，且更易于大规模生产（在印刷机发明之前，所有记谱音乐均为手抄）。乐谱的普及使音乐风格传播得更快、范围更广。乐师和歌手常为教会、宫廷和城镇服务。教会合唱团规模扩大，教会仍是音乐的重要赞助者。到15世纪中叶，作曲家们创作了丰富的复调宗教音乐，其中不同旋律线同时交织。这一时期的杰出作曲家包括纪尧姆·迪费、乔瓦尼·皮耶路易吉·达·帕莱斯特里纳、托马斯·莫利、奥兰多·迪·拉索和若斯坎·德·普雷。随着音乐活动从教会转向贵族宫廷，国王、王后和王子们竞相争夺最优秀的作曲家。许多一流作曲家来自尼德兰、比利时和法国，他们被称为法兰德—佛兰德作曲家。他们在整个欧洲，尤其是意大利，占据重要职位。其他音乐活动活跃的国家包括德国、英国和西班牙。

04

Common practice period

Baroque

The Baroque era of music took place from 1600 to 1750, coinciding with the flourishing of the Baroque artistic style in Europe. The start of the Baroque era was marked by the penning of the first operas. Polyphonic contrapuntal music (music with separate, simultaneous melodic lines) remained important during this period. German Baroque composers wrote for small ensembles including strings, brass, and woodwinds, as well as for choirs and keyboard instruments such as pipe organ, harpsichord, and clavichord. Musical complexity increased during this time. Several major musical forms were created, some of them which persisted into later periods, seeing further development. These include the fugue, the invention, the sonata, and the concerto. The late Baroque style was polyphonically complex and richly ornamented. Important composers from the Baroque era include Johann Sebastian Bach (Cello suites), George Frideric Handel (Messiah), Georg Philipp Telemann, and Antonio Vivaldi (The Four Seasons).

通奏低音时期

巴洛克

巴洛克音乐时期为1600年至1750年，与欧洲巴洛克艺术风格的繁荣时期相重合。巴洛克时期的开端以第一批歌剧的创作为标志。复调对位音乐（具有独立且同时进行的多条旋律线的音乐）在这一时期仍然占据重要地位。德国巴洛克作曲家为小型合奏团创作，包括弦乐、铜管和木管乐器，以及合唱团和管风琴、羽管键琴、击弦古钢琴等键盘乐器。这一时期的音乐复杂度不断提升。若干主要音乐体裁在此时诞生，其中一些延续至后世并得到进一步发展，包括赋格、创意曲、奏鸣曲和协奏曲。晚期巴洛克风格在复调上极为复杂，且装饰华丽。巴洛克时期的重要作曲家包括约翰·塞巴斯蒂安·巴赫（无伴奏大提琴组曲）、乔治·弗里德里希·亨德尔（《弥赛亚》）、格奥尔格·菲利普·泰勒曼，以及安东尼奥·维瓦尔第（《四季》）。

04

Classicism

The music of the Classical period (1730 to 1820) aimed to imitate what were seen as the key elements of the art and philosophy of Ancient Greece and Rome: the ideals of balance, proportion, and disciplined expression. Music from the Classical period has a lighter, clearer, and considerably simpler texture than the Baroque music which preceded it. The main style was homophony, where a prominent melody and a subordinate chordal accompaniment part are clearly distinct. Classical instrumental melodies tended to be almost voicelike and singable. New genres were developed, and the fortepiano, the forerunner to the modern piano, replaced the Baroque era harpsichord and pipe organ as the main keyboard instrument (though pipe organ continued to be used in sacred music, such as Masses).

古典主义

古典主义时期（1730至1820年）的音乐旨在模仿古希腊和古罗马艺术与哲学中被视为核心的要素：平衡、比例与克制表达的理想。古典主义时期的音乐比之前的巴洛克音乐在织体上更轻盈、更清晰，也更为简洁。主要风格为主调音乐，即一条突出的旋律与一个从属的和声伴奏部分清晰分明。古典主义时期的器乐旋律倾向于接近人声，易于演唱。新的音乐体裁不断涌现，而早期钢琴——现代钢琴的前身——取代了巴洛克时期的羽管键琴和管风琴，成为主要的键盘乐器（不过管风琴在宗教音乐如弥撒中仍继续使用）。

04

Importance was given to instrumental music. It was dominated by further development of musical forms initially defined in the Baroque period: the sonata, the concerto, and the symphony. Other main kinds were the trio, string quartet, serenade, and divertimento. The sonata was the most important and developed form. Although Baroque composers also wrote sonatas, the Classical style of sonata is completely distinct. All of the main instrumental forms of the Classical era, from string quartets to symphonies and concertos, were based on the structure of the sonata. The instruments used chamber music and orchestra became more standardized. In place of the basso continuo group of the Baroque era, which consisted of harpsichord, organ, or lute along with a number of bass instruments selected at the discretion of the group leader (e.g., viol, cello, theorbo, serpent), Classical chamber groups used specified, standardized instruments (e.g., a string quartet would be performed by two violins, a viola, and a cello). The practice of improvised chord-playing by the continuo keyboardist or lute player, a hallmark of Baroque music, underwent a gradual decline between 1750 and 1800.

器乐音乐受到高度重视，其发展主要围绕巴洛克时期初步确立的音乐体裁展开：奏鸣曲、协奏曲和交响曲。其他主要体裁包括三重奏、弦乐四重奏、小夜曲和嬉游曲。奏鸣曲是最重要且发展最充分的体裁。尽管巴洛克作曲家也写过奏鸣曲，但古典主义风格的奏鸣曲与之截然不同。古典时期所有主要器乐体裁，从弦乐四重奏到交响曲和协奏曲，均以奏鸣曲的结构为基础。室内乐和管弦乐队所使用的乐器趋于标准化。取代巴洛克时期通奏低音组（由羽管键琴、管风琴或鲁特琴加上由领队自由选择的若干低音乐器如维奥尔琴、大提琴、西奥伯琴、蛇形号组成）的是古典主义室内乐组所使用的明确、标准化的乐器编制（例如弦乐四重奏由两把小提琴、一把中提琴和一把大提琴演奏）。巴洛克音乐标志性的通奏低音乐器即兴和弦演奏做法在1750至1800年间逐渐衰落。

04

One of the most important changes made in the Classical period was the development of public concerts. The aristocracy still played a significant role in the sponsorship of concerts and compositions, but it was now possible for composers to survive without being permanent employees of queens or princes. The increasing popularity of classical music led to a growth in the number and types of orchestras. The expansion of orchestral concerts necessitated the building of large public performance spaces. Symphonic music including symphonies, musical accompaniment to ballet, and mixed vocal/instrumental genres, such as opera and oratorio, became more popular.

古典主义时期最重要的变革之一是公开音乐会的发展。贵族在赞助音乐会和作曲方面仍发挥着重要作用，但作曲家此时已无需作为女王或王子的终身雇员即可谋生。古典音乐日益普及，推动了管弦乐队数量和类型的增长。管弦乐音乐会的扩展催生了大型公共演出场所的建设。交响性音乐——包括交响曲、芭蕾舞配乐，以及歌剧和清唱剧等声乐与器乐混合体裁——变得更加流行。

04

The best known composers of Classicism are Carl Philipp Emanuel Bach, Christoph Willibald Gluck, Johann Christian Bach, Joseph Haydn, Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Ludwig van Beethoven, and Franz Schubert. Beethoven and Schubert are also considered to be composers in the later part of the Classical era, as it began to move towards Romanticism.

古典主义最著名的作曲家包括卡尔·菲利普·埃马努埃尔·巴赫、克里斯托夫·维利巴尔德·格鲁克、约翰·克里斯蒂安·巴赫、约瑟夫·海顿、沃尔夫冈·阿马德乌斯·莫扎特、路德维希·凡·贝多芬和弗朗茨·舒伯特。贝多芬和舒伯特也被视为古典主义后期的作曲家，因为这一时期已开始向浪漫主义过渡。

04

Romanticism

Romantic music (c. 1820 to 1900) from the 19th century had many elements in common with the Romantic styles in literature and painting of the era. Romanticism was an artistic, literary, and intellectual movement was characterized by its emphasis on emotion and individualism as well as glorification of all the past and nature. Romantic music expanded beyond the rigid styles and forms of the Classical era into more passionate, dramatic, and expressive pieces and songs. Romantic composers such as Wagner and Brahms attempted to increase emotional expression and power in their music to describe deeper truths or human feelings. With symphonic tone poems, composers tried to tell stories and evoke images or landscapes using instrumental music. Some composers promoted nationalistic pride with patriotic orchestral music inspired by folk music. The emotional and expressive qualities of music came to take precedence over tradition.

浪漫主义

19世纪的浪漫主义音乐（约1820至1900年）与同时期文学和绘画中的浪漫主义风格有许多共同之处。浪漫主义是一场艺术、文学与思想运动，其特征在于强调情感与个人主义，以及对过去和自然的崇尚。浪漫主义音乐突破了古典主义时期严谨的风格与形式，转向更具激情、戏剧性和表现力的作品与歌曲。瓦格纳和勃拉姆斯等浪漫主义作曲家试图在音乐中增强情感表达与力量，以描绘更深层的真理或人类情感。借助交响音诗，作曲家们尝试用器乐讲述故事、唤起画面或描绘风景。部分作曲家以受民间音乐启发的爱国管弦乐来弘扬民族自豪感。音乐的情感性与表现性开始超越传统而占据首要地位。

04

Romantic composers grew in idiosyncrasy, and went further in the syncretism of exploring different art-forms in a musical context, (such as literature), history (historical figures and legends), or nature itself. Romantic love or longing was a prevalent theme in many works composed during this period. In some cases, the formal structures from the classical period continued to be used (e.g., the sonata form used in string quartets and symphonies), but these forms were expanded and altered. In many cases, new approaches were explored for existing genres, forms, and functions. Also, new forms were created that were deemed better suited to the new subject matter. Composers continued to develop opera and ballet music, exploring new styles and themes.

浪漫主义作曲家的个人风格愈发鲜明，并在融合不同艺术形式方面走得更远——他们在音乐中融入文学、历史（历史人物与传说）乃至自然本身等元素。浪漫爱情或渴望是这一时期许多作品中的常见主题。在某些情况下，古典主义时期的正式结构仍被沿用（如弦乐四重奏和交响曲中使用的奏鸣曲式），但这些形式得到了扩展和改造。在许多情况下，作曲家们为既有体裁、形式和功能探索了新的处理方式。同时，也诞生了一些被认为更适合新题材的新形式。作曲家们继续发展歌剧和芭蕾舞音乐，探索新的风格与主题。

04

In the years after 1800, the music developed by Ludwig van Beethoven and Franz Schubert introduced a more dramatic, expressive style. In Beethoven’s case, short motifs, developed organically, came to replace melody as the most significant compositional unit (an example is the distinctive four note figure used in his Fifth Symphony). Later Romantic composers such as Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky, Antonín Dvořák, and Gustav Mahler used more unusual chords and more dissonance to create dramatic tension. They generated complex and often much longer musical works. During the late Romantic period, composers explored dramatic chromatic alterations of tonality, such as extended chords and altered chords, which created new sound “colors.” The late 19th century saw a dramatic expansion in the size of the orchestra, and the Industrial Revolution helped to create better instruments, creating a more powerful sound. Public concerts became an important part of well-to-do urban society. It also saw a new diversity in theatre music, including operetta, and musical comedy and other forms of musical theatre.

1800年以后，路德维希·凡·贝多芬和弗朗茨·舒伯特所发展的音乐引入了更具戏剧性和表现力的风格。就贝多芬而言，经过有机发展的短小动机取代了旋律，成为最重要的作曲单位（典型例子是他的《第五交响曲》中那个标志性的四音符动机）。后来的浪漫主义作曲家如彼得·伊里奇·柴可夫斯基、安东宁·德沃夏克和古斯塔夫·马勒，运用更为罕见的和弦与更多的不协和音来营造戏剧张力。他们创作了结构复杂且篇幅通常更长的音乐作品。在浪漫主义晚期，作曲家们探索了调性的剧烈半音变化，如扩展和弦与变化和弦，从而创造出新的音响”色彩”。19世纪末，管弦乐队的规模急剧扩大，而工业革命则推动了更精良乐器的制造，带来了更为强大的音响效果。公开音乐会成为富裕城市社会生活的重要组成部分。这一时期的戏剧音乐也呈现出新的多样性，包括轻歌剧、音乐喜剧及其他形式的音乐剧。

20th and 21st century

04

In the 19th century, a key way new compositions became known to the public was by the sales of sheet music, which middle class amateur music lovers would perform at home, on their piano or other common instruments, such as the violin. With 20th-century music, the invention of new electric technologies such as radio broadcasting and mass market availability of gramophone records meant sound recordings heard by listeners (on the radio or record player) became the main way to learn about new songs and pieces. There was a vast increase in music listening as the radio gained popularity and phonographs were used to replay and distribute music; anyone with a radio or record player could hear operas, symphonies, and big bands in their own living room. During the 19th century, the focus on sheet music had restricted access to new music to middle and upper-class people who could read music and who owned pianos and other instruments. Radios and record players allowed lower-income people, who could not afford an opera or symphony concert ticket, to hear this music. As well, people could hear music from different parts of the country, or even different parts of the world, even if they could not afford to travel to these locations. This helped to spread musical styles.

19世纪，新作品为公众所知的主要途径是乐谱销售，中产阶级业余音乐爱好者会在家中用钢琴或小提琴等常见乐器演奏这些乐谱。到了20世纪，收音机广播等新电气技术的发明以及留声机唱片的大规模市场普及，意味着听众通过收音机或唱机听到的录音成为了解新歌曲和新作品的主要方式。随着收音机的普及和留声机被用于回放与传播音乐，音乐聆听量大幅增长；任何拥有收音机或唱机的人都可以在自家客厅里听到歌剧、交响曲和大乐队的演奏。19世纪，乐谱的主导地位使新音乐的获取局限于能够识谱且拥有钢琴等乐器的中上层阶级。而收音机和唱机使无力购买歌剧或交响音乐会门票的低收入人群也能聆听到这些音乐。此外，人们即使无力亲身前往，也能听到来自本国其他地区乃至世界各地的音乐。这有助于音乐风格的传播。

04

The focus of art music in the 20th century was characterized by exploration of new rhythms, styles, and sounds. The horrors of World War I influenced many of the arts, including music, and composers began exploring darker, harsher sounds. Traditional music styles such as jazz and folk music were used by composers as a source of ideas for classical music. Igor Stravinsky, Arnold Schoenberg, and John Cage were influential composers in 20th-century art music. The invention of sound recording and the ability to edit music gave rise to new subgenres of classical music, including the acousmatic and musique concrète schools of electronic composition. Sound recording was a major influence on the development of popular music genres, because it enabled recordings of songs and bands to be widely distributed. The introduction of the multitrack recording system had a major influence on rock music, because it could do more than record a band’s performance. Using a multitrack system, a band and their music producer could overdub many layers of instrument tracks and vocals, creating new sounds that would not be possible in a live performance.

20世纪艺术音乐的核心特征在于对新节奏、新风格和新音响的探索。第一次世界大战的惨烈影响了包括音乐在内的众多艺术领域，作曲家们开始探索更阴暗、更粗粝的音响。爵士乐和民间音乐等传统音乐风格被作曲家们用作古典音乐的灵感来源。伊戈尔·斯特拉文斯基、阿诺德·勋伯格和约翰·凯奇是20世纪艺术音乐中具有影响力的作曲家。录音技术的发明以及音乐编辑能力的出现催生了古典音乐的新分支，包括电子作曲中的具体音乐学派和音乐具体派。录音技术对流行音乐体裁的发展产生了重大影响，因为它使歌曲和乐队的录音得以广泛传播。多轨录音系统的引入对摇滚乐产生了深远影响，因为它的功能远不止记录乐队的现场演奏。借助多轨系统，乐队及其音乐制作人可以叠录多层乐器轨和人声，创造出在现场演奏中无法实现的新音响。

04

Jazz evolved and became an important genre of music over the course of the 20th century, and during the second half, rock music did the same. Jazz is an American musical artform that originated in the late 19th and early 20th centuries in African American communities in the Southern United States from a confluence of African and European music traditions. The style’s West African pedigree is evident in its use of blue notes, improvisation, polyrhythms, syncopation, and the swung note.

爵士乐在20世纪不断演变，成为一种重要的音乐体裁；而在20世纪下半叶，摇滚乐也走上了同样的道路。爵士乐是一种美国音乐艺术形式，起源于19世纪末至20世纪初美国南部的非裔美国人社区，融合了非洲与欧洲的音乐传统。其西非血统在蓝调音、即兴演奏、复节奏、切分音和摇摆音的运用中清晰可见。

04

Rock music is a genre of popular music that developed in the 1950s from rock and roll, rockabilly, blues, and country music. The sound of rock often revolves around the electric or acoustic guitar, and it uses a strong back beat laid down by a rhythm section. Along with the guitar or keyboards, saxophone and blues-style harmonica are used as soloing instruments. In its “purest form”, it “has three chords, a strong, insistent back beat, and a catchy melody.” The traditional rhythm section for popular music is rhythm guitar, electric bass guitar, and drums. Some bands have keyboard instruments such as organ, piano, or, since the 1970s, analog synthesizers. In the 1980s, pop musicians began using digital synthesizers, such as the DX-7 synthesizer, electronic drum machines such as the TR-808, and synth bass devices (such as the TB-303) or synth bass keyboards. In the 1990s, an increasingly large range of computerized hardware musical devices and instruments and software (e.g. digital audio workstations) were used. In the 2020s, soft synths and computer music apps make it possible for bedroom producers to create and record types of music, such as electronic dance music, in their home, adding sampled and digital instruments and editing the recording digitally. In the 1990s, bands in genres such as nu metal began including DJs in their bands. DJs create music by manipulating recorded music, using a DJ mixer.

摇滚乐是一种流行音乐体裁，于20世纪50年代由摇滚乐、山地摇滚、布鲁斯和乡村音乐发展而来。摇滚乐的音响通常以电吉他或原声吉他为核心，并由节奏组提供强劲的反拍。除吉他或键盘外，萨克斯管和布鲁斯风格的口琴也常用作独奏乐器。在其”最纯粹的形式”中，它”有三个和弦、强劲而持续的反拍，以及一段朗朗上口的旋律。”流行音乐的传统节奏组包括节奏吉他、电贝斯和鼓。部分乐队配有风琴、钢琴等键盘乐器，或自20世纪70年代起使用模拟合成器。20世纪80年代，流行音乐人开始使用数字合成器（如DX-7合成器）、电子鼓机（如TR-808）以及合成贝斯设备（如TB-303）或合成贝斯键盘。20世纪90年代，越来越多的计算机化硬件乐器、设备和软件（如数字音频工作站）被投入使用。到了2020年代，软件合成器和计算机音乐应用使卧室制作人能够在家中创作和录制电子舞曲等音乐类型，他们添加采样和数字乐器，并以数字方式编辑录音。20世纪90年代，新金属等体裁的乐队开始在乐队中加入DJ。DJ通过操作录音音乐并使用DJ混音台来创作音乐。

Creation

04

Composition

“Composition” is the act or practice of creating a song, an instrumental music piece, a work with both singing and instruments, or another type of music. In many cultures, including Western classical music, the act of composing also includes the creation of music notation, such as a sheet music “score”, which is then performed by the composer or by other singers or musicians. In popular music and traditional music, the act of composing, which is typically called songwriting, may involve the creation of a basic outline of the song, called the lead sheet, which sets out the melody, lyrics, and chord progression. In classical music, the composer typically orchestrates their own compositions, but in musical theatre and in pop music, songwriters may hire an arranger to do the orchestration. In some cases, songwriters may not use notation at all, and instead compose the song in their minds and then play or record it from memory. In jazz and popular music, notable recordings by influential performers are given the weight that written scores play in classical music.

作曲

“作曲”是指创作歌曲、器乐曲、声乐与器乐结合的作品或其他类型音乐的行为或实践。在包括西方古典音乐在内的许多文化中，作曲行为还包括音乐记谱的创作，如乐谱”总谱”，随后由作曲家本人或其他歌手、乐手演奏。在流行音乐和传统音乐中，作曲行为通常称为”写歌”，可能涉及创作一份歌曲的基本框架，即主旋律谱（lead sheet），其中列出旋律、歌词与和弦进行。在古典音乐中，作曲家通常亲自为自己的作品编配管弦乐，但在音乐剧和流行音乐中，词曲作者可能会聘请编曲人来完成编配。在某些情况下，词曲作者可能完全不使用记谱，而是在脑中完成作曲，再凭记忆演奏或录音。在爵士乐和流行音乐中，有影响力的表演者的经典录音所具有的分量，相当于古典音乐中书面总谱的地位。

04

Even when music is notated relatively precisely, as in classical music, there are many decisions that a performer has to make, because notation does not specify all of the elements of music precisely. The process of deciding how to perform music that has been previously composed and notated is termed “interpretation”. Different performers’ interpretations of the same work of music can vary widely, in terms of the tempos that are chosen and the playing or singing style or phrasing of the melodies. Composers and songwriters who present their own music are interpreting their songs, just as much as those who perform the music of others. The standard body of choices and techniques present at a given time and a given place is referred to as performance practice, whereas interpretation is generally used to mean the individual choices of a performer.

即使音乐被相对精确地记谱，如古典音乐，演奏者仍需做出许多决定，因为记谱无法精确规定音乐的所有要素。决定如何演奏已被作曲并记谱的音乐的过程称为”诠释”。不同演奏者对同一部音乐作品的诠释可能差异极大，体现在所选择的速度、演奏或演唱风格以及旋律的分句处理上。呈现自己音乐的作曲家和词曲作者同样是在诠释自己的歌曲，正如演奏他人音乐的表演者一样。在特定时间和特定地点所存在的一套标准选择与技巧被称为”演奏实践”，而”诠释”一词通常用来指表演者的个人选择。

04

Although a musical composition often uses musical notation and has a single author, this is not always the case. A work of music can have multiple composers, which often occurs in popular music when a band collaborates to write a song, or in musical theatre, when one person writes the melodies, a second person writes the lyrics, and a third person orchestrates the songs. In some styles of music, such as the blues, a composer/songwriter may create, perform and record new songs or pieces without ever writing them down in music notation. A piece of music can also be composed with words, images, or computer programs that explain or notate how the singer or musician should create musical sounds. Examples range from avant-garde music that uses graphic notation, to text compositions such as Aus den sieben Tagen, to computer programs that select sounds for musical pieces. Music that makes heavy use of randomness and chance is called aleatoric music, and is associated with contemporary composers active in the 20th century, such as John Cage, Morton Feldman, and Witold Lutosławski. A commonly known example of chance-based music is the sound of wind chimes jingling in a breeze.

尽管音乐作品常使用音乐记谱法，且通常只有一位作者，但情况并非总是如此。一部音乐作品可以有多个作曲家，这常见于流行音乐中乐队合作写歌，或在音乐剧中，一人写旋律，另一人写歌词，第三人进行配器。在某些音乐风格中，例如蓝调，作曲家或歌曲创作者可能创作、表演并录制新歌或乐曲，而根本不用音乐符号将其记录下来。一首乐曲也可以用文字、图像或计算机程序来创作，这些文字、图像或程序解释或指明歌手或音乐家应如何创造音乐声音。例子包括使用图形记谱法的前卫音乐，到像《来自七天》这样的文本作品，再到为音乐作品选择声音的计算机程序。大量运用随机性和偶然性的音乐被称为偶然音乐，与活跃于20世纪的当代作曲家如约翰·凯奇、莫顿·费尔德曼和维托尔德·卢托斯瓦夫斯基联系在一起。一个广为人知的基于偶然性的音乐例子是风铃在微风中叮当作响的声音。

04

The study of composition has traditionally been dominated by examination of methods and practice of Western classical music, but the definition of composition is broad enough to include the creation of popular music and traditional music songs and instrumental pieces as well as spontaneously improvised works like those of free jazz performers and African percussionists such as Ewe drummers.

作曲研究传统上一直以对西方古典音乐方法和实践的考察为主导，但作曲的定义足够广泛，可以包括流行音乐和传统音乐歌曲及器乐作品的创作，也包括那些自发即兴创作的作品，如自由爵士乐表演者和非洲打击乐手（如埃维鼓手）的作品。

Performance

04

Performance is the physical expression of music, which occurs when a song is sung or played. In classical music, a work is written in music notation by a composer and then performed once the composer is satisfied with its structure and instrumentation. However, as it gets performed, the interpretation of a song or piece can evolve and change. In classical music, instrumental performers, singers or conductors may gradually make changes to the phrasing or tempo of a piece. In popular and traditional music, the performers have more freedom to make changes to the form of a song or piece. As such, in popular and traditional music styles, even when a band plays a cover song, they can make changes such as adding a guitar solo or inserting an introduction.

表演是音乐的身体表达，当一首歌曲被演唱或一首乐曲被演奏时发生。在古典音乐中，作品由作曲家以音乐符号写成，一旦作曲家对其结构和配器感到满意，就会被表演。然而，随着作品的表演，对歌曲或乐曲的诠释可以演变和改变。在古典音乐中，器乐演奏者、歌手或指挥可能会逐渐改变乐曲的分句或速度。在流行音乐和传统音乐中，表演者有更多的自由来改变歌曲或乐曲的形式。因此，在流行音乐和传统音乐风格中，即使一个乐队演奏一首翻唱歌曲，他们也可以做出改变，例如加入吉他独奏或插入引子。

04

A performance can either be planned out and rehearsed (practiced)—which is the norm in classical music, jazz big bands, and many popular music styles—or improvised over a chord progression (a sequence of chords), which is the norm in small jazz and blues groups. Rehearsals of orchestras, concert bands and choirs are led by a conductor. Rock, blues and jazz bands are usually led by the bandleader. A rehearsal is a structured repetition of a song or piece by the performers until it can be sung or played correctly and, if it is a song or piece for more than one musician, until the parts are together from a rhythmic and tuning perspective.

表演可以是事先规划并排练（练习）过的——这在古典音乐、爵士大乐队和许多流行音乐风格中是常态——也可以是在一个和弦进行（一连串和弦）上即兴演奏，这在小型的爵士和蓝调乐队中是常态。管弦乐队、管乐团和合唱团的排练由指挥带领。摇滚、蓝调和爵士乐队通常由乐队领队带领。排练是表演者对一首歌曲或乐曲进行结构化的重复，直到能够正确地演唱或演奏，并且如果是多于一人的歌曲或乐曲，还要达到各部分在节奏和音准上协调一致。

04

Many cultures have strong traditions of solo performance (in which one singer or instrumentalist performs), such as in Indian classical music, and in the Western art-music tradition. Other cultures, such as in Bali, include strong traditions of group performance. All cultures include a mixture of both, and performance may range from improvised solo playing to highly planned and organized performances such as the modern classical concert, religious processions, classical music festivals, or music competitions. Chamber music, which is music for a small ensemble with only one or a few of each type of instrument, is often seen as more intimate than large symphonic works.

许多文化都有深厚的独奏传统（即由一位歌手或器乐演奏者表演），例如在印度古典音乐和西方艺术音乐传统中。其他文化，例如巴厘岛，则有深厚的合奏传统。所有文化都包含两者的混合，表演范围可以从即兴独奏到高度计划和组织的表演，如现代古典音乐会、宗教游行、古典音乐节或音乐比赛。室内乐是为小型乐团创作的音乐，每种乐器只有一件或几件，通常被认为比大型交响乐作品更亲切。

Improvisation

04

Musical improvisation is the creation of spontaneous music, often within (or based on) a pre-existing harmonic framework, chord progression, or riffs. Improvisers use the notes of the chord, various scales that are associated with each chord, and chromatic ornaments and passing tones which may be neither chord tones nor from the typical scales associated with a chord. Musical improvisation can be done with or without preparation. Improvisation is a major part of some types of music, such as blues, jazz, and jazz fusion, in which instrumental performers improvise solos, melody lines, and accompaniment parts.

音乐即兴是创造自发的音乐，通常是在（或基于）一个预先存在的和声框架、和弦进行或连复段内进行。即兴演奏者使用和弦音、与每个和弦相关的各种音阶，以及可能既非和弦音也非来自与和弦相关的典型音阶的半音装饰音和经过音。音乐即兴可以有准备也可以没有准备。即兴是某些音乐类型（如蓝调、爵士乐和融合爵士乐）的重要组成部分，在这些音乐中，器乐演奏者即兴演奏独奏、旋律线和伴奏部分。

04

In the Western art music tradition, improvisation was an important skill during the Baroque era and during the Classical era. In the Baroque era, performers improvised ornaments, and basso continuo keyboard players improvised chord voicings based on figured bass notation. As well, the top soloists were expected to be able to improvise pieces such as preludes. In the Classical era, solo performers and singers improvised virtuoso cadenzas during concerts.

在西方艺术音乐传统中，即兴在巴洛克时期和古典主义时期是一项重要技能。在巴洛克时期，表演者即兴演奏装饰音，通奏低音键盘手则根据数字低音符号即兴演奏和弦音型。此外，顶尖的独奏者也被期望能够即兴演奏前奏曲等作品。在古典主义时期，独奏演奏者和歌手在音乐会中即兴演奏炫技性的华彩乐段。

04

However, in the 20th and early 21st century, as “common practice” Western art music performance became institutionalized in symphony orchestras, opera houses, and ballets, improvisation has played a smaller role, as more and more music was notated in scores and parts for musicians to play. At the same time, some 20th and 21st century art music composers have increasingly included improvisation in their creative work. In Indian classical music, improvisation is a core component and an essential criterion of performances.

然而，在20世纪和21世纪初，随着”通用实践”的西方艺术音乐表演在交响乐团、歌剧院和芭蕾舞团中变得制度化，即兴发挥的作用越来越小，因为越来越多的音乐被标记在乐谱和分谱中供音乐家演奏。与此同时，一些20和21世纪的艺术音乐作曲家在他们的创作作品中越来越多地加入即兴元素。在印度古典音乐中，即兴是核心组成部分，也是表演的基本评判标准。

Art and entertainment

04

Music is composed and performed for many purposes, ranging from aesthetic pleasure, religious or ceremonial purposes, or as an entertainment product for the marketplace. When music was only available through sheet music scores, such as during the Classical and Romantic eras, music lovers would buy the sheet music of their favourite pieces and songs so that they could perform them at home on the piano. With the advent of the phonograph, records of popular songs, rather than sheet music became the dominant way that music lovers would enjoy their favourite songs. With the advent of home tape recorders in the 1980s and digital music in the 1990s, music lovers could make tapes or playlists of favourite songs and take them with them on a portable cassette player or MP3 player. Some music lovers create mix tapes of favourite songs, which serve as a “self-portrait, a gesture of friendship, prescription for an ideal party… an environment consisting solely of what is most ardently loved”.

音乐的创作和表演有多种目的，范围包括审美愉悦、宗教或仪式目的，或作为面向市场的娱乐产品。当音乐只能通过乐谱获得时，例如在古典主义和浪漫主义时期，音乐爱好者会购买他们最喜欢的作品和歌曲的乐谱，以便在家中的钢琴上演奏。随着留声机的出现，流行歌曲的唱片，而非乐谱，成为音乐爱好者欣赏他们喜爱歌曲的主要方式。随着20世纪80年代家用磁带录音机和20世纪90年代数字音乐的出现，音乐爱好者可以制作他们喜爱的歌曲的磁带或播放列表，并用便携式卡带播放器或MP3播放器随身携带。一些音乐爱好者会制作包含最爱歌曲的混音带，它们充当着”自画像、友谊的姿态、理想派对的处方……一个完全由最热爱之物组成的环境”。

04

Amateur musicians can compose or perform music for their own pleasure and derive income elsewhere. Professional musicians are employed by institutions and organisations, including armed forces (in marching bands, concert bands, and popular music groups), religious institutions, symphony orchestras, broadcasting or film production companies, and music schools. Professional musicians sometimes work as freelancers or session musicians, seeking contracts and engagements in a variety of settings. There are often many links between amateur and professional musicians. Beginning amateur musicians take lessons with professional musicians. In community settings, advanced amateur musicians perform with professional musicians in a variety of ensembles such as community concert bands and community orchestras.

业余音乐家可以为自娱自乐而创作或表演音乐，并从其他渠道获取收入。专业音乐家受雇于各种机构和组织，包括军队（在军乐队、管乐团和流行音乐团体中）、宗教机构、交响乐团、广播或电影制作公司，以及音乐学校。专业音乐家有时作为自由职业者或录音室乐手工作，在各种场合寻找合同和演出机会。业余和专业音乐家之间通常存在许多联系。初学的业余乐手会跟随专业音乐家上课。在社区环境中，高水平的业余乐手会与专业音乐家在各种合奏团中表演，如社区管乐团和社区管弦乐团。

04

A distinction is often made between music performed for a live audience and music that is performed in a studio so that it can be recorded and distributed through the music retail system or the broadcasting system. However, there are also many cases where a live performance in front of an audience is also recorded and distributed. Live concert recordings are popular in both classical music and in popular music forms such as rock, where illegally taped live concerts are prized by music lovers. In the jam band scene, live, improvised jam sessions are preferred to studio recordings.

通常，为现场观众表演的音乐与在录音室表演以便录制并通过音乐零售系统或广播系统发行的音乐之间有所区别。然而，也有许多在现场观众面前表演同时也被录制和发行的情况。现场音乐会录音在古典音乐和诸如摇滚等流行音乐形式中都很受欢迎，在摇滚乐中，非法录制的现场音乐会被音乐爱好者珍视。在即兴乐队圈子里，现场的、即兴的即兴演奏会比录音室录音更受青睐。

Notation

04

Music notation typically means the written expression of music notes and rhythms on paper using symbols. When music is written down, the pitches and rhythm of the music, such as the notes of a melody, are notated. Music notation often provides instructions on how to perform the music. For example, the sheet music for a song may state the song is a “slow blues” or a “fast swing”, which indicates the tempo and the genre. To read notation, a person must have an understanding of music theory, harmony and the performance practice associated with a particular song or piece’s genre.

音乐记谱法通常指用符号在纸上书面表达音符和节奏。当音乐被写下时，音乐的音高和节奏，例如旋律的音符，被记录下来。音乐记谱法通常提供如何表演音乐的指示。例如，一首歌的乐谱可能会标明这首歌是”慢速蓝调”或”快速摇摆”，这表明了速度和风格流派。要阅读乐谱，一个人必须理解乐理、和声以及与特定歌曲或乐曲风格相关的表演实践。

04

Written notation varies with the style and period of music. Nowadays, notated music is produced as sheet music or, for individuals with computer scorewriter programs, as an image on a computer screen. In ancient times, music notation was put onto stone or clay tablets. To perform music from notation, a singer or instrumentalist requires an understanding of the rhythmic and pitch elements embodied in the symbols and the performance practice that is associated with a piece of music or genre. In genres requiring musical improvisation, the performer often plays from music where only the chord changes and form of the song are written, requiring the performer to have a great understanding of the music’s structure, harmony, and the styles of a particular genre e.g., jazz or country music.

书面记谱法因音乐的风格和时期而异。如今，被记谱的音乐制作成纸本乐谱，或者对于使用计算机记谱软件的个人来说，以图像形式显示在计算机屏幕上。在古代，音乐记谱法被刻在石头或泥板上。要根据乐谱表演音乐，歌手或器乐演奏者需要理解符号中蕴含的节奏和音高元素，以及与一首乐曲或流派相关的表演实践。在需要音乐即兴的流派中，表演者通常根据只写有和弦变化和歌曲形式的乐谱来演奏，这要求表演者对音乐的结构、和声以及特定流派（例如爵士乐或乡村音乐）的风格有深刻的理解。

04

In Western art music, the most common types of written notation are scores, which include all the music parts of an ensemble piece, and parts, which are the music notation for the individual performers or singers. In popular music, jazz, and blues, the standard musical notation is the lead sheet, which notates the melody, chords, lyrics (if it is a vocal piece), and structure of the music. Fake books are also used in jazz; they may consist of lead sheets or simply chord charts, which permit rhythm section members to improvise an accompaniment part to jazz songs. Scores and parts are also used in popular music and jazz, particularly in large ensembles such as jazz “big bands.” In popular music, guitarists and electric bass players often read music notated in tablature (often abbreviated as “tab”), which indicates the location of the notes to be played on the instrument using a diagram of the guitar or bass fingerboard. Tablature was used in the Baroque era to notate music for the lute, a stringed, fretted instrument.

在西方艺术音乐中，最常见的书面记谱类型是总谱，它包含合奏作品的所有音乐声部，以及分谱，是为单个演奏者或歌手记录的音乐符号。在流行音乐、爵士乐和蓝调中，标准的音乐记谱法是领谱，它记录了旋律、和弦、歌词（如果是声乐作品）和音乐结构。爵士乐中也使用仿谱集；它们可能由领谱或简单的和弦图表组成，允许节奏声部成员为爵士歌曲即兴伴奏。总谱和分谱也用于流行音乐和爵士乐，特别是在大型合奏团中，如爵士”大乐队”。在流行音乐中，吉他手和电贝司手经常阅读用指法谱（通常缩写为”tab”）记录的音乐，这种谱用吉他或贝斯指板的图示来指明在乐器上演奏音符的位置。指法谱在巴洛克时期用于记录琉特琴（一种有品弦乐器）的音乐。

Oral and aural tradition

04

Many types of music, such as traditional blues and folk music were not written down in sheet music; instead, they were originally preserved in the memory of performers, and the songs were handed down orally, from one musician or singer to another, or aurally, in which a performer learns a song “by ear”. When the composer of a song or piece is no longer known, this music is often classified as “traditional” or as a “folk song”. Different musical traditions have different attitudes towards how and where to make changes to the original source material, from quite strict, to those that demand improvisation or modification to the music. A culture’s history and stories may also be passed on by ear through song.

许多类型的音乐，例如传统蓝调和民间音乐，并未以乐谱形式记录下来；相反，它们最初保存在表演者的记忆中，歌曲通过口头方式从一位音乐家或歌手传给另一位，或者通过听觉方式，即表演者通过”用耳朵听”来学习歌曲。当一首歌或乐曲的作曲家已不再为人所知时，这种音乐通常被归类为”传统音乐”或”民歌”。不同的音乐传统对于如何以及在何处修改原始素材材料持有不同的态度，有的相当严格，有的则要求即兴或修改音乐。一个文化的历史和故事也可能通过歌曲以听觉方式代代相传。

Elements

04

Music has many different fundamentals or elements. Depending on the definition of “element” being used, these can include pitch, beat or pulse, tempo, rhythm, melody, harmony, texture, style, allocation of voices, timbre or color, dynamics, expression, articulation, form, and structure. The elements of music feature prominently in the music curriculums of Australia, the UK, and the US. All three curriculums identify pitch, dynamics, timbre, and texture as elements, but the other identified elements of music are far from universally agreed upon. Below is a list of the three official versions of the “elements of music”:

音乐有许多不同的基本要素或元素。根据所使用的”元素”定义，这些可以包括音高、节拍或脉动、速度、节奏、旋律、和声、织体、风格、声部分配、音色或色彩、力度、表达、运音法、形式与结构。音乐元素在澳大利亚、英国和美国的音乐课程中占有突出地位。这三个课程体系都认定音高、力度、音色和织体为元素，但其他公认的音乐元素则远未达成统一。以下是三种官方版本”音乐元素”的列表：

The phrase “the elements of music” is used in a number of different contexts. The two most common contexts can be differentiated by describing them as the “rudimentary elements of music” and the “perceptual elements of music”.

“音乐元素”这个短语在多种不同的语境中使用。两种最常见的语境可以通过将其描述为”音乐的基础元素”和”音乐的感知元素”来加以区分。

Pitch

04

Pitch is an aspect of a sound that we can hear, reflecting whether one musical sound, note, or tone is “higher” or “lower” than another musical sound, note, or tone. We can talk about the highness or lowness of pitch in the more general sense, such as the way a listener hears a piercingly high piccolo note or whistling tone as higher in pitch than a deep thump of a bass drum. We also talk about pitch in the precise sense associated with musical melodies, basslines, and chords. Precise pitch can only be determined in sounds that have a frequency that is clear and stable enough to distinguish from noise. For example, it is much easier for listeners to discern the pitch of a single note played on a piano than to try to discern the pitch of a crash cymbal that is struck.

音高是我们可以听到的声音的一个方面，反映了某个音乐声音、音符或音调比另一个音乐声音、音符或音调是”更高”还是”更低”。我们可以在更普遍的意义上谈论音高的高低，比如听者听到尖利刺耳的短笛声或口哨声比低音鼓的深沉敲击声在音高上更高。我们也在与音乐旋律、低音线和和弦相关的精确意义上谈论音高。精确的音高只能在那些具有足够清晰稳定的频率以区别于噪音的声音中确定。例如，听者辨别钢琴上弹奏的单个音符的音高，远比试图辨别击打吊镲的音高要容易得多。

Melody

04

A melody, also called a “tune”, is a series of pitches (notes) sounding in succession (one after the other), often in a rising and falling pattern. The notes of a melody are typically created using pitch systems such as scales or modes. Melodies also often contain notes from the chords used in the song. The melodies in simple folk songs and traditional songs may use only the notes of a single scale, the scale associated with the tonic note or key of a given song. For example, a folk song in the key of C (also referred to as C major) may have a melody that uses only the notes of the C major scale (the individual notes C, D, E, F, G, A, B, and C; these are the “white notes” on a piano keyboard. On the other hand, Bebop-era jazz from the 1940s and contemporary music from the 20th and 21st centuries may use melodies with many chromatic notes (i.e., notes in addition to the notes of the major scale; on a piano, a chromatic scale would include all the notes on the keyboard, including the “white notes” and “black notes” and unusual scales, such as the whole tone scale (a whole tone scale in the key of C would contain the notes C, D, E, F♯, G♯, and A♯). A low musical line played by bass instruments, such as double bass, electric bass, or tuba, is called a bassline.

旋律，也称为”曲调”，是一系列音高（音符）连续发声（一个接一个），通常以起伏的模式进行。旋律的音符通常是使用音高系统（如音阶或调式）创建的。旋律也常包含歌曲所用和弦中的音符。简单的民歌和传统歌曲中的旋律可能只使用单一音阶的音符，该音阶与给定歌曲的主音或调性相关。例如，一首C大调（也称为C大调）的民歌，其旋律可能只使用C大调音阶的音符（即各音符C、D、E、F、G、A、B和C；这些是钢琴键盘上的”白键音”）。另一方面，20世纪40年代的比博普时代爵士乐以及20和21世纪的当代音乐可能使用带有许多半音（即，除了大调音阶音符之外的音符；在钢琴上，半音阶将包括键盘上的所有音符，包括”白键音”和”黑键音”）和不寻常音阶（例如全音音阶；C大调的全音音阶将包含音符C、D、E、F♯、G♯和A♯）的旋律。由低音乐器（如低音提琴、电贝司或大号）演奏的低音乐句称为低音线。

Harmony

04

Harmony refers to the “vertical” sounds of pitches in music, which means pitches that are played or sung together at the same time creates a chord. Usually, this means the notes are played at the same time, although harmony may also be implied by a melody that outlines a harmonic structure (i.e., by using melody notes that are played one after the other, outlining the notes of a chord). In music written using the system of major-minor tonality (“keys”), which includes most classical music written from 1600 to 1900 and most Western pop, rock, and traditional music, the key of a piece determines the “home note” or tonic to which the piece generally resolves, and the character (e.g. major or minor) of the scale in use. Simple classical pieces and many pop and traditional music songs are written so that all the music is in a single key. More complex Classical, pop, and traditional music songs and pieces may have two keys (and in some cases three or more keys). Classical music from the Romantic era (written from about 1820–1900) often contains multiple keys, as does jazz, especially Bebop jazz from the 1940s, in which the key or “home note” of a song may change every four bars or even every two bars.

和声指的是音乐中音高的”纵向”声音，即意味着同时演奏或演唱的音高可以创造一个和弦。通常，这意味着音符是同时演奏的，尽管和声也可以通过勾勒和声结构的旋律来暗示（即，通过使用一个接一个演奏的旋律音来勾勒和弦的音符）。在使用大小调调性（”调”）体系创作的音乐中——这包括1600年至1900年间创作的大多数古典音乐，以及大多数西方流行、摇滚和传统音乐——一首乐曲的调性决定了作品通常要解决到的”主音”或主音，以及所用音阶的性质（例如大调或小调）。简单的古典作品以及许多流行和传统音乐歌曲都写成所有音乐都在单一调性内。更复杂的古典、流行和传统音乐歌曲和作品可能有两个调（在某些情况下有三个或更多调）。浪漫主义时期（约1820-1900年）的古典音乐通常包含多个调，爵士乐也是如此，尤其是20世纪40年代的比博普爵士乐，其中歌曲的调性或”主音”可能每四个小节甚至每两个小节就改变。

Rhythm

04

Rhythm is the arrangement of sounds and silences in time. Meter animates time in regular pulse groupings, called measures or bars, which in Western classical, popular, and traditional music often group notes in sets of two (e.g., 2/4 time), three (e.g., 3/4 time, also known as Waltz time or 3/8 time), or four (e.g., 4/4 time). Meters are made easier to hear because songs and pieces often (but not always) place an emphasis on the first beat of each grouping. Notable exceptions exist, such as the backbeat used in much Western pop and rock, in which a song that uses a measure that consists of four beats (called 4/4 time or common time) will have accents on beats two and four, which are typically performed by the drummer on the snare drum, a loud and distinctive-sounding percussion instrument. In pop and rock, the rhythm parts of a song are played by the rhythm section, which includes chord-playing instruments (e.g., electric guitar, acoustic guitar, piano, or other keyboard instruments), a bass instrument (typically electric bass or for some styles such as jazz and bluegrass, double bass) and a drum kit player.

节奏是声音与静默在时间中的安排。节拍以规则的脉动分组来活跃时间，称为小节，在西方古典、流行和传统音乐中，通常将音符按两拍一组（例如，2/4拍）、三拍一组（例如，3/4拍，也称为圆舞曲拍，或3/8拍）或四拍一组（例如，4/4拍）进行分组。由于歌曲和作品通常（但并非总是）将重音放在每一组的第一个拍点上，节拍因此变得更容易听辨。也存在显著的例外，例如在大量西方流行和摇滚乐中使用的反拍，一首采用四拍一小节（称为4/4拍或普通拍）的歌曲，会在第二和第四拍上加重音，这些重音通常由鼓手在军鼓（一种声音响亮且独特的打击乐器）上演奏。在流行和摇滚乐中，歌曲的节奏部分由节奏声部演奏，包括和弦乐器（例如电吉他、原声吉他、钢琴或其他键盘乐器）、低音乐器（通常是电贝司，或某些风格如爵士乐和蓝草音乐中的低音提琴）以及架子鼓手。

Texture

04

Musical texture is the overall sound of a piece of music or song. The texture of a piece or song is determined by how the melodic, rhythmic, and harmonic materials are combined in a composition, thus determining the overall nature of the sound in a piece. Texture is often described in regard to the density, or thickness, and range, or width, between lowest and highest pitches, in relative terms as well as more specifically distinguished according to the number of voices, or parts, and the relationship between these voices (see common types below). For example, a thick texture contains many ‘layers’ of instruments. One layer can be a string section or another brass. The thickness is affected by the amount and the richness of the instruments. Texture is commonly described according to the number of and relationship between parts or lines of music:

音乐织体是一首乐曲或歌曲的整体声音。一首作品或歌曲的织体，取决于旋律、节奏与和声材料在作品中的组合方式，从而决定了一部作品中声音的整体性质。织体通常根据密度或厚度，以及最低和最高音高之间的范围或宽度来相对描述，同时更具体地根据声部或音部的数量以及这些声部之间的关系来区分（见下文常见类型）。例如，厚的织体包含许多乐器”层”。一层可以是弦乐组，另一层是铜管组。厚度受到乐器数量和丰富程度的影响。织体通常根据音乐声部或线条的数量及其关系来描述：

04

monophony: a single melody (or “tune”) with neither instrumental accompaniment nor a harmony part. A mother singing a lullaby to her baby would be an example.
heterophony: two or more instruments or singers playing/singing the same melody, but with each performer slightly varying the rhythm or speed of the melody or adding different ornaments to the melody. Two bluegrass fiddlers playing the same traditional fiddle tune together will typically each vary the melody by some degree and each add different ornaments.
polyphony: multiple independent melody lines that interweave together, which are sung or played at the same time. Choral music written in the Renaissance music era was typically written in this style. A round, which is a song such as “Row, Row, Row Your Boat”, which different groups of singers all start to sing at a different time, is an example of polyphony.
homophony: a clear melody supported by chordal accompaniment. Most Western popular music songs from the 19th century onward are written in this texture.
Music that contains a large number of independent parts (e.g., a double concerto accompanied by 100 orchestral instruments with many interweaving melodic lines) is generally said to have a “thicker” or “denser” texture than a work with few parts (e.g., a solo flute melody accompanied by a single cello).

单声部织体：单一的旋律（或”曲调”），既无乐器伴奏，也无和声声部。一位母亲给她的宝宝唱摇篮曲就是一个例子。
异声部织体：两个或多个乐器或歌手演奏/演唱同一旋律，但每位表演者轻微地改变旋律的节奏或速度，或添加不同的装饰音。两位蓝草音乐小提琴手一起演奏同一首传统小提琴曲调时，通常各自会对旋律进行某种程度的改变，并各自添加不同的装饰音。
复调织体：多个独立的旋律线条交织在一起，同时演唱或演奏。文艺复兴音乐时期创作的合唱音乐通常以这种风格写成。轮唱曲，例如《划，划，划你的船》这首歌，不同组的歌手在不同时间开始演唱，就是复调的一个例子。
主调织体：一条清晰的旋律，由和弦伴奏支撑。19世纪以来的大多数西方流行音乐歌曲都以这种织体写成。
包含大量独立声部的音乐（例如，由100件管弦乐器伴奏、具有许多交织旋律线的双协奏曲）通常被认为比声部少的作品（例如，由一把大提琴伴奏的独奏长笛旋律）具有”更厚”或”更密”的织体。

Timbre

04

Timbre, sometimes called “color” or “tone color” is the quality or sound of a voice or instrument. Timbre is what makes a particular musical sound different from another, even when they have the same pitch and loudness. For example, a 440 Hz A note sounds different when it is played on oboe, piano, violin, or electric guitar. Even if different players of the same instrument play the same note, their notes might sound different due to differences in instrumental technique (e.g., different embouchures), different types of accessories (e.g., mouthpieces for brass players, reeds for oboe and bassoon players), or strings made out of different materials for string players (e.g., gut strings versus steel strings). Even two instrumentalists playing the same note on the same instrument (one after the other) may sound different due to different ways of playing the instrument (e.g., two string players might hold the bow differently).

音色，有时称为”色彩”或”音品”，是人声或乐器的品质或声音。音色是使一个特定的音乐声音区别于另一个声音的特质，即使它们具有相同的音高和响度。例如，一个440赫兹的A音在双簧管、钢琴、小提琴或电吉他上演奏时听起来不同。即使同一乐器的不同演奏者演奏同一个音符，他们的音符也可能由于乐器演奏技巧（例如，不同的口型）、不同类型的配件（例如，铜管乐手的号嘴，双簧管和巴松管吹奏者的簧片）或弦乐演奏者使用不同材料制成的琴弦（例如，羊肠弦对钢丝弦）而听起来不同。甚至两个器乐演奏者先后在同一件乐器上演奏同一个音符，也可能因为演奏乐器的方式不同（例如，两位弦乐演奏者可能持弓方式不同）而发出不同的声音。

04

The physical characteristics of sound that determine the perception of timbre include the spectrum, envelope, and overtones of a note or musical sound. For electric instruments developed in the 20th century, such as electric guitar, electric bass, and electric piano, the performer can also change the tone by adjusting equalizer controls, tone controls on the instrument, and by using electronic effects units such as distortion pedals. The tone of the electric Hammond organ is controlled by adjusting drawbars.

决定音色感知的声音物理特性包括一个音符或音乐声音的频谱、包络和泛音。对于20世纪开发的电子乐器，例如电吉他、电贝司和电钢琴，演奏者还可以通过调整均衡器控制、乐器上的音调控制，以及使用诸如失真踏板等电子效果器来改变音色。电子哈蒙德风琴的音色是通过调节拉杆来控制的。

Expression

04

Expressive qualities are those elements in music that create change in music without changing the main pitches or substantially changing the rhythms of the melody and its accompaniment. Performers, including singers and instrumentalists, can add musical expression to a song or piece by adding phrasing, by adding effects such as vibrato (with voice and some instruments, such as guitar, violin, brass instruments, and woodwinds), dynamics (the loudness or softness of piece or a section of it), tempo fluctuations (e.g., ritardando or accelerando, which are, respectively slowing down and speeding up the tempo), by adding pauses or fermatas on a cadence, and by changing the articulation of the notes (e.g., making notes more pronounced or accented, by making notes more legato, which means smoothly connected, or by making notes shorter).

表现品质是音乐中那些在不改变主要音高或实质性改变旋律及其伴奏节奏的情况下，在音乐中创造变化的元素。表演者，包括歌手和器乐演奏者，可以通过添加分句、添加如颤吟等效果（用于人声和一些乐器，如吉他、小提琴、铜管乐器和木管乐器）、力度（乐曲或其一个段落的响亮或柔和程度）、速度波动（例如渐慢或渐快，即分别放慢和加快速度）、在终止式上添加停顿或延长记号，以及改变音符的运音法（例如，使音符更突出或加重音，使音符更连奏，即平滑连接，或使音符更短促）来为歌曲或作品增添音乐表达。

04

Expression is achieved through the manipulation of pitch (such as inflection, vibrato, slides, etc.), volume (dynamics, accent, tremolo, etc.), duration (tempo fluctuations, rhythmic changes, changing note duration such as with legato and staccato, etc.), timbre (e.g. changing vocal timbre from a light to a resonant voice), and sometimes even texture (e.g. doubling the bass note for a richer effect in a piano piece). Expression therefore can be seen as a manipulation of all elements to convey “an indication of mood, spirit, character etc.” and as such cannot be included as a unique perceptual element of music, although it can be considered an important rudimentary element of music.

表达是通过对音高（如音高弯曲、颤吟、滑音等）、音量（力度、重音、震音等）、时值（速度波动、节奏变化、改变音符时值如连奏和断奏等）、音色（例如将声乐音色从轻盈变为响亮的声音），有时甚至是织体（例如在钢琴曲中加倍低音音符以获得更丰富的效果）的操控来实现的。因此，表达可以被视为对所有元素的操控，以传达”一种情绪、精神、性格等的指示”，因此不能作为音乐的一个独特的感知元素，尽管它可以被视为音乐的一个重要基础元素。

Form

04

In music, form describes the overall structure or plan of a song or piece of music, and it describes the layout of a composition as divided into sections. In the early 20th century, Tin Pan Alley songs and Broadway musical songs were often in AABA thirty-two-bar form, in which the A sections repeated the same eight bar melody (with variation) and the B section provided a contrasting melody or harmony for eight bars. From the 1960s onward, Western pop and rock songs are often in verse-chorus form, which comprises a sequence of verse and chorus (“refrain”) sections, with new lyrics for most verses and repeating lyrics for the choruses. Popular music often makes use of strophic form, sometimes in conjunction with the twelve bar blues.

在音乐中，形式描述了歌曲或音乐作品的整体结构或计划，它描述了一部作品被划分为多个部分的布局。在20世纪早期，叮砰巷歌曲和百老汇音乐剧歌曲通常采用AABA三十二小节形式，其中A段重复相同的八小节旋律（带有变化），B段提供对比性的旋律或和声，也为八小节。从20世纪60年代起，西方流行和摇滚歌曲通常采用主歌-副歌形式，包括一系列主歌和副歌（”叠句”）段落，大部分主歌有新歌词，副歌则重复歌词。流行音乐常使用分节歌形式，有时与十二小节蓝调结合使用。

04

In the tenth edition of The Oxford Companion to Music, Percy Scholes defines musical form as “a series of strategies designed to find a successful mean between the opposite extremes of unrelieved repetition and unrelieved alteration.” Examples of common forms of Western music include the fugue, the invention, sonata-allegro, canon, strophic, theme and variations, and rondo.

在《牛津音乐指南》第十版中，珀西·斯科尔斯将音乐形式定义为”一系列策略，旨在找到介于无休止的重复和无休止的变化这两个对立极端之间的成功中庸之道。”西方音乐常见形式的例子包括赋格曲、创意曲、奏鸣曲-快板、卡农、分节歌、主题与变奏以及回旋曲。

04

Scholes states that European classical music had only six stand-alone forms: simple binary, simple ternary, compound binary, rondo, air with variations, and fugue (although musicologist Alfred Mann emphasized that the fugue is primarily a method of composition that has sometimes taken on certain structural conventions.)

斯科尔斯指出，欧洲古典音乐只有六种独立形式：简单二部曲式、简单三部曲式、复合二部曲式、回旋曲式、变奏曲式（主题与变奏）和赋格曲（尽管音乐学家阿尔弗雷德·曼强调，赋格主要是一种作曲方法，有时会呈现某些结构惯例。）

04

Where a piece cannot readily be broken into sectional units (though it might borrow some form from a poem, story, or programme), it is said to be through-composed. Such is often the case with a fantasia, prelude, rhapsody, etude (or study), symphonic poem, Bagatelle, impromptu, or similar composition. Professor Charles Keil classified forms and formal detail as “sectional, developmental, or variational.”

如果一部作品不能轻易地分解为段落单元（尽管它可能借用了诗歌、故事或标题内容的一些形式），则称其为通谱体。幻想曲、前奏曲、狂想曲、练习曲、交响诗、小品曲、即兴曲或类似作品通常属于这种情况。查尔斯·凯尔教授将形式和形式细节分类为”段落式、展开式或变奏式”。

Philosophy

04

The philosophy of music is the study of fundamental questions regarding music and has connections with questions in metaphysics and aesthetics. Questions include:

音乐哲学是对关于音乐的基本问题的研究，与形而上学和美学中的问题有关联。问题包括：

04

What is the definition of music? (What are the necessary and sufficient conditions for classifying something as music?)
What is the relationship between music and mind?
What does music history reveal to us about the world?
What is the connection between music and emotions?
What is meaning in relation to music?
In ancient times, such as with the Ancient Greeks, the aesthetics of music explored the mathematical and cosmological dimensions of rhythmic and harmonic organization. In the 18th century, focus shifted to the experience of hearing music, and thus to questions about its beauty and human enjoyment (plaisir and jouissance) of music. The origin of this philosophic shift is sometimes attributed to Alexander Gottlieb Baumgarten in the 18th century, followed by Immanuel Kant. Through their writing, the ancient term ‘aesthetics’, meaning sensory perception, received its present-day connotation. In the 2000s, philosophers have tended to emphasize issues besides beauty and enjoyment. For example, music’s capacity to express emotion has been foregrounded.

音乐的定义是什么？（将某物归类为音乐的必要且充分条件是什么？）
音乐与心灵之间的关系是什么？
音乐史向我们揭示了世界的什么？
音乐与情感之间的联系是什么？
与音乐相关的意义是什么？
在古代，例如古希腊人时期，音乐美学探索了节奏与和声组织的数学和宇宙维度。到了18世纪，焦点转向了聆听音乐的体验，因而转向了关于音乐之美和人类从音乐中获得的愉悦与享乐的问题。这一哲学转变的起源有时归因于18世纪的亚历山大·戈特利布·鲍姆嘉通，随后是伊曼努尔·康德。通过他们的著作，意为感官知觉的古老术语”美学”获得了其现今的内涵。在21世纪，哲学家们倾向于强调美和愉悦之外的问题。例如，音乐表达情感的能力被置于突出地位。

04

In the 20th century, important contributions were made by Peter Kivy, Jerrold Levinson, Roger Scruton, and Stephen Davies. However, many musicians, music critics, and other non-philosophers have contributed to the aesthetics of music. In the 19th century, a significant debate arose between Eduard Hanslick, a music critic and musicologist, and composer Richard Wagner regarding whether music can express meaning. Harry Partch and some other musicologists, such as Kyle Gann, have studied and tried to popularize microtonal music and the usage of alternate musical scales. Modern composers like La Monte Young, Rhys Chatham, and Glenn Branca paid much attention to a scale called just intonation.

在20世纪，彼得·基维、杰罗德·莱文森、罗杰·斯克鲁顿和斯蒂芬·戴维斯做出了重要贡献。然而，许多音乐家、音乐评论家和其他非哲学家也为音乐美学做出了贡献。19世纪，音乐评论家兼音乐学家爱德华·汉斯力克与作曲家理查德·瓦格纳之间就音乐能否表达意义展开了一场重大辩论。哈里·帕奇以及其他一些音乐学家，如凯尔·甘恩，研究并试图普及微分音音乐和替代音阶的使用。像拉·蒙特·杨、里斯·查塔姆和格伦·布兰卡等现代作曲家对一种称为纯律的音阶给予了很多关注。

04

It is often thought that music has the ability to affect our emotions, intellect, and psychology; it can assuage our loneliness or incite our passions. Plato suggests in The Republic that music has a direct effect on the soul. Therefore, he proposes that in the ideal regime music would be closely regulated by the state (Book VII). In Ancient China, Confucius believed that music and rituals or rites are interconnected and harmonious with nature; he stated that music was the harmonization of heaven and earth, while the order was brought by the rites order, making them extremely crucial functions in society.

人们通常认为音乐有能力影响我们的情感、智力和心理；它可以缓解我们的孤独感或激发我们的热情。柏拉图在《理想国》中提出，音乐对灵魂有直接的影响。因此，他提议在理想的政体中，音乐应由国家严格管制（第七卷）。在古代中国，孔子认为音乐与礼仪或典礼是相互关联的，并与自然和谐一致；他说音乐是天地和谐的表现，而秩序则由礼制带来，使它们在社会中发挥至关重要的功能。

Psychology

04

Modern music psychology aims to explain and understand musical behavior and experience. Research in this field and its subfields are primarily empirical; their knowledge tends to advance on the basis of interpretations of data collected by systematic observation of and interaction with human participants. In addition to its focus on fundamental perceptions and cognitive processes, music psychology is a field of research with practical relevance for many areas, including music performance, composition, education, criticism, and therapy, as well as investigations of human aptitude, skill, intelligence, creativity, and social behavior.

现代音乐心理学旨在解释和理解音乐行为和体验。该领域及其子领域的研究主要是实证性的；它们的知识倾向于基于对通过系统观察人类参与者并与之互动收集的数据的解释而推进。除了关注基本感知和认知过程外，音乐心理学还是一个对许多领域具有实际意义的研究领域，包括音乐表演、作曲、教育、评论和治疗，以及对人类能力倾向、技能、智力、创造力和社会行为的研究。

Neuroscience

04

Cognitive neuroscience of music is the scientific study of brain-based mechanisms involved in the cognitive processes underlying music. These behaviours include music listening, performing, composing, reading, writing, and ancillary activities. It also is increasingly concerned with the brain basis for musical aesthetics and musical emotion. The field is distinguished by its reliance on direct observations of the brain, using such techniques as functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI), transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS), magnetoencephalography (MEG), electroencephalography (EEG), and positron emission tomography (PET).

音乐认知神经科学是对音乐背后认知过程所涉及的基于大脑的机制进行的科学研究。这些行为包括聆听音乐、表演、作曲、阅读、写作以及辅助活动。它也越来越多地关注音乐美学和音乐情感的大脑基础。该领域的特点是依赖于对大脑的直接观察，使用诸如功能性磁共振成像、经颅磁刺激、脑磁图、脑电图和正电子发射断层扫描等技术。

Cognitive musicology

04

Cognitive musicology is a branch of cognitive science concerned with computationally modeling musical knowledge with the goal of understanding both music and cognition. The use of computer models provides an exacting, interactive medium in which to formulate and test theories and has roots in artificial intelligence and cognitive science.

认知音乐学是认知科学的一个分支，致力于通过计算机构建音乐知识的模型，其目标是理解音乐和认知。计算机模型的使用提供了一个严谨、互动的媒介来制定和检验理论，并植根于人工智能和认知科学。

04

Cognitive musicology investigates topics such as the parallels between language and music in the brain. Research often includes biologically inspired models of computation, such as neural networks and evolutionary programs. This field seeks to model how musical knowledge is represented, stored, perceived, performed, and generated. By using a well-structured computer environment, the systematic structures of these cognitive phenomena can be investigated.

认知音乐学研究诸如大脑中语言和音乐之间的相似之处等课题。研究常包括生物学启发的计算模型，例如神经网络和进化程序。该领域试图模拟音乐知识是如何被表征、储存、感知、表演和生成的。通过使用结构良好的计算机环境，可以研究这些认知现象的系统结构。

Psychoacoustics

04

Psychoacoustics is the scientific study of sound perception. More specifically, it is the branch of science studying the psychological and physiological responses associated with sound (including speech and music). It can be further categorized as a branch of psychophysics.

心理声学是对声音感知的科学研究。更具体地说，它是研究声音（包括言语和音乐）相关的心理和生理反应的科学分支。它可以进一步归类为心理物理学的一个分支。

Evolutionary musicology

04

Evolutionary musicology concerns the “origins of music, the question of animal song, selection pressures underlying music evolution”, and “music evolution and human evolution”. It seeks to understand music perception and activity in the context of evolutionary theory. Charles Darwin speculated that music may have held an adaptive advantage and functioned as a protolanguage, a view which has spawned several competing theories of music evolution. An alternate view sees music as a by-product of linguistic evolution; a type of “auditory cheesecake” that pleases the senses without providing any adaptive function. This view has been directly countered by numerous music researchers.

进化音乐学关注”音乐的起源、动物鸣叫声的问题、驱动音乐进化的选择压力”以及”音乐进化与人类进化”。它试图在进化论的背景下理解音乐感知和活动。查尔斯·达尔文推测，音乐可能具有适应性优势，并充当原始语言，这一观点催生了几个相互竞争的音乐进化理论。另一种观点认为，音乐是语言进化的副产品；一种”听觉芝士蛋糕”，只愉悦感官而不提供任何适应功能。这种观点遭到了众多音乐研究者的直接反驳。

Cultural effects

04

An individual’s culture or ethnicity plays a role in their music cognition, including their preferences, emotional reaction, and musical memory. Musical preferences are biased toward culturally familiar musical traditions beginning in infancy, and adults’ classification of the emotion of a musical piece depends on both culturally specific and universal structural features. Additionally, individuals’ musical memory abilities are greater for culturally familiar music than for culturally unfamiliar music.

个人的文化或种族在其音乐认知中扮演着重要角色，包括他们的偏好、情感反应和音乐记忆。音乐偏好从婴儿期就开始偏向文化上熟悉的音乐传统，成年人对于一首音乐作品情感的分类，同时取决于文化特定的和普遍的结构特征。此外，个人对文化上熟悉的音乐的记忆能力，比文化上不熟悉的音乐更强。

Perceptual

04

Since the emergence of the study of psychoacoustics in the 1930s, most lists of elements of music have related more to how we hear music than how we learn to play it or study it. C.E. Seashore, in his book Psychology of Music, identified four “psychological attributes of sound”. These were: “pitch, loudness, time, and timbre” (p. 3). He did not call them the “elements of music” but referred to them as “elemental components” (p. 2). Nonetheless, these elemental components link precisely with four of the most common musical elements: “Pitch” and “timbre” match exactly, “loudness” links with dynamics, and “time” links with the time-based elements of rhythm, duration, and tempo. This usage of the phrase “the elements of music” links more closely with Webster’s New 20th Century Dictionary definition of an element as: “a substance which cannot be divided into a simpler form by known methods” and educational institutions’ lists of elements generally align with this definition as well.

自20世纪30年代心理声学研究出现以来，大多数音乐元素的列表更多地涉及我们如何听音乐，而不是我们如何学习演奏或研究它。C.E. 西肖尔在其著作《音乐心理学》中，确定了”声音的四个心理属性”。它们是：”音高、响度、时间和音色”（第3页）。他没有称它们为”音乐元素”，而是称其为”基本成分”（第2页）。尽管如此，这些基本成分精确地与四个最常见的音乐元素相链接：”音高”和”音色”完全匹配，”响度”与力度相链接，”时间”与基于时间的节奏、时值和速度元素相链接。这种对”音乐元素”短语的用法，更接近《韦氏新20世纪词典》对元素的定义：”一种无法通过已知方法分割成更简单形式的物质”，并且教育机构的元素列表通常也符合这个定义。

04

Although writers of lists of “rudimentary elements of music” can vary their lists depending on their personal (or institutional) priorities, the perceptual elements of music should consist of an established (or proven) list of discrete elements which can be independently manipulated to achieve an intended musical effect. It seems at this stage that there is still research to be done in this area.

尽管撰写”音乐基础元素”列表的作者可以根据个人（或机构）的优先考虑而变化其列表，但音乐的感知元素应该包含一个既定的（或已证实的）离散元素列表，这些元素可以被独立操控以达到预期的音乐效果。现阶段看来，这一领域仍有待研究。

04

A slightly different way of approaching the identification of the elements of music, is to identify the “elements of sound” as: pitch, duration, loudness, timbre, sonic texture, and spatial location, and then to define the “elements of music” as: sound, structure, and artistic intent.

一种识别音乐元素的略为不同的方法是，首先确定”声音的元素”为：音高、时值、响度、音色、声音织体和空间位置，然后将”音乐的元素”定义为：声音、结构和艺术意图。

Sociological aspects

04

Ethnographic studies demonstrate that music is a participatory, community-based activity. Music is experienced by individuals in a range of social settings from being alone, to attending a large concert, forming a music community, which cannot be understood as a function of individual will or accident; it includes both commercial and non-commercial participants with a shared set of common values. Musical performances take different forms in different cultures and socioeconomic milieus.

人种志研究表明，音乐是一种参与性的、基于社区的活动。个人在一系列社会环境中体验音乐，从独处到参加大型音乐会，形成音乐社区，这不能被理解为个人意愿或偶然事件的结果；它包含了具有一套共同价值观的商业和非商业参与者。音乐表演在不同文化和社会经济环境中呈现出不同的形式。

04

In Europe and North America, there was a divide between what types of music were viewed as “high culture” and “low culture”. “High culture” included Baroque, Classical, Romantic, and modern-era symphonies, concertos, and solo works, and are typically heard in formal concerts in concert halls and churches, with the audience sitting quietly. Other types of music—including jazz, blues, soul, and country—are often performed in bars, nightclubs, and theatres, where the audience may drink, dance, and cheer. Until the 20th century, the division between “high” and “low” musical forms was accepted as a valid distinction that separated out “art music” from popular music heard in bars and dance halls. Musicologists, such as David Brackett, note a “redrawing of high-low cultural-aesthetic boundaries” in the 20th century. And, “when industry and public discourses link categories of music with categories of people, they tend to conflate stereotypes with actual listening communities.” Stereotypes can be based on socioeconomic standing, or social class, of the performers or audience of the different types of music.

在欧洲和北美，人们曾将音乐类型划分为”高雅文化”和”低俗文化”。”高雅文化”包括巴洛克、古典、浪漫以及现代的交响乐、协奏曲和独奏作品，通常在音乐厅和教堂的正式音乐会上聆听，观众安静就座。其他音乐类型——包括爵士乐、蓝调、灵魂乐和乡村音乐——常在酒吧、夜总会和剧院演出，观众可以饮酒、跳舞和欢呼。直到20世纪，”高雅”和”低俗”音乐形式之间的划分，被视为区分酒吧和舞厅中听到的”艺术音乐”与流行音乐的有效区别。音乐学家，如大卫·布拉克特，注意到20世纪”高雅-低俗文化审美边界的重划”。并且，”当产业和公共话语将音乐类别与人群类别联系起来时，它们倾向于将刻板印象与实际听众群体混为一谈”。刻板印象可能基于不同类型音乐的表演者或听众的社会经济地位或社会阶层。

04

When composers introduce styles of music that break with convention, there can be strong resistance from academics and others. Late-period Beethoven string quartets, Stravinsky ballet scores, serialism, bebop, hip-hop, punk rock, and electronica were controversial and criticised, when they were first introduced. Such themes are examined in the sociology of music, sometimes called sociomusicology, which is pursued in departments of sociology, media studies, or music, and is closely related to ethnomusicology.

当作曲家引入打破常规的音乐风格时，可能会遭到学术界和其他方面的强烈抵制。贝多芬晚期的弦乐四重奏、斯特拉文斯基的芭蕾舞剧配乐、序列主义、比博普、嘻哈、朋克摇滚和电子乐，在它们首次被引入时都引起了争议和批评。这类主题在音乐社会学（有时称为社会音乐学）中进行研究，该学科在社会学、媒体研究或音乐系中开展，并与民族音乐学密切相关。

Role of women

04

Women have played a major role in music throughout history, as composers, songwriters, instrumental performers, singers, conductors, music scholars, music educators, music critics/music journalists, and other musical professions. In the 2010s, while women comprised a significant proportion of popular music and classical music singers, and a significant proportion of songwriters (many of them being singer-songwriters), there were few women record producers, rock critics, and rock instrumentalists. Although there have been a huge number of women composers in classical music, from the medieval period to the present day, women composers have been significantly underrepresented in the commonly performed classical music repertoire, music history textbooks, and music encyclopedias; for example, in the Concise Oxford History of Music, Clara Schumann is one of the few female composers who is mentioned.

纵观历史，女性在音乐领域扮演了重要角色，作为作曲家、歌曲创作者、器乐演奏者、歌手、指挥家、音乐学者、音乐教育者、音乐评论家/音乐记者以及其他音乐职业。在2010年代，虽然女性在流行音乐和古典音乐歌手以及歌曲作者中占了相当大的比例（其中许多人是创作型歌手），但女性唱片制作人、摇滚评论家和摇滚乐器演奏者却很少。尽管从中世纪时期至今，古典音乐领域已有大量女性作曲家，但在常演的古典音乐曲目、音乐史教科书和音乐百科全书中，女性作曲家的代表性明显不足；例如，在《简明牛津音乐史》中，克拉拉·舒曼是少数被提及的女性作曲家之一。

04

Women comprise a significant proportion of instrumental soloists in classical music and the percentage of women in orchestras is increasing. A 2015 article on concerto soloists in major Canadian orchestras, however, indicated that 84% of the soloists with the Montreal Symphony Orchestra were men. In 2012, women still made up just 6% of the top-ranked Vienna Philharmonic orchestra. Women are less common as instrumental players in popular music genres such as rock and heavy metal, although there have been a number of notable female instrumentalists and all-female bands. Women are particularly underrepresented in extreme metal genres. In the 1960s pop-music scene, “ike most aspects of the…music business, songwriting was a male-dominated field. Though there were plenty of female singers on the radio, women …were primarily seen as consumers:… Singing was sometimes an acceptable pastime for a girl, but playing an instrument, writing songs, or producing records simply wasn’t done.” Young women “…were not socialized to see themselves as people who create .”

女性在古典音乐的器乐独奏者中占有相当的比例，管弦乐团中女性的比例也在增加。然而，2015年一篇关于加拿大主要管弦乐团协奏曲独奏者的文章指出，蒙特利尔交响乐团84%的独奏者是男性。2012年，女性在顶级排名的维也纳爱乐乐团中仍仅占6%。在摇滚和重金属等流行音乐流派中，女性作为器乐演奏者不太常见，尽管已有多位著名的女性器乐演奏家和全女子乐队。女性在极端金属流派中的代表性尤其不足。在20世纪60年代的流行乐坛，”就像…音乐行业的许多方面一样，歌曲创作是一个男性主导的领域。尽管收音机里有大量的女歌手，女性…主要被视为消费者：…唱歌有时对女孩来说是可接受的消遣，但演奏乐器、写歌或制作唱片根本就不被考虑。”年轻女性”…没有在社交中被培养成视自己为（音乐）创造者的人。”

04

Women are also underrepresented in orchestral conducting, music criticism/music journalism, music producing, and sound engineering. While women were discouraged from composing in the 19th century, and there are few women musicologists, women became involved in music education “…to such a degree that women dominated during the later half of the 19th century and well into the 20th century.”

女性在管弦乐指挥、音乐评论/音乐新闻、音乐制作和音响工程领域的代表性也不足。虽然在19世纪不鼓励女性作曲，而且女性音乐学家很少，但女性开始参与到音乐教育中”…达到了这样的程度，以至于在19世纪后半叶直至20世纪，女性（在此领域）占据了主导地位。”

04

According to Jessica Duchen, a music writer for London’s The Independent, women musicians in classical music are “…too often judged for their appearances, rather than their talent” and they face pressure “…to look sexy onstage and in photos.” Duchen states that while “here are women musicians who refuse to play on their looks,…the ones who do tend to be more materially successful.” According to the UK’s Radio 3 editor, Edwina Wolstencroft, the music industry has long been open to having women in performance or entertainment roles, but women are much less likely to have positions of authority, such as being the conductor of an orchestra. In popular music, while there are many women singers recording songs, there are very few women behind the audio console acting as music producers, the individuals who direct and manage the recording process. One of the most recorded artists is Asha Bhosle, an Indian singer best known as a playback singer in Hindi cinema.

据伦敦《独立报》的音乐作家杰西卡·杜琴称，古典音乐界的女性音乐家”……太常因外表而非才华受到评判”，并且她们面临着”……在舞台上和照片中要看起来性感的压力。” 杜琴指出，虽然”有些女音乐家拒绝靠外貌演奏，…那些愿意这样做的人往往在物质上更成功。” 据英国广播公司第三台编辑埃德温娜·沃尔斯滕克罗夫特称，音乐行业长期以来一直对女性担任表演或娱乐角色持开放态度，但女性担任权威职位（如管弦乐团指挥）的可能性要小得多。在流行音乐中，虽然有许多女歌手录制歌曲，但很少有女性在音频控制台后担任音乐制作人，即那些指导和管理录音过程的人。录制作品最多的艺术家之一是阿莎·波斯雷，她是一位以印地语电影中的幕后配唱歌手身份著称的印度歌手。

Media and technology

04

Since the 20th century, live music can be broadcast over the radio, television, or the Internet, or recorded and listened to on a CD player or MP3 player.

自20世纪以来，现场音乐可以通过广播、电视或互联网播出，或者录制下来在CD播放器或MP3播放器上聆听。

04

In the early 20th century (in the late 1920s), as talking pictures emerged in the early 20th century, with their prerecorded musical tracks, an increasing number of moviehouse orchestra musicians found themselves out of work. During the 1920s, live musical performances by orchestras, pianists, and theater organists were common at first-run theaters. With the coming of the talking motion pictures, those featured performances were largely eliminated. The American Federation of Musicians (AFM) took out newspaper advertisements protesting the replacement of live musicians with mechanical playing devices. One 1929 ad that appeared in the Pittsburgh Press features an image of a can labeled “Canned Music / Big Noise Brand / Guaranteed to Produce No Intellectual or Emotional Reaction Whatever”

在20世纪早期（20世纪20年代末），随着有声电影及其预录音轨的出现，越来越多的电影院管弦乐团乐手发现自己失业了。在20世纪20年代，管弦乐团、钢琴家和剧院管风琴师的现场音乐表演在首轮影院很常见。随着有声电影的来临，那些特色表演基本被淘汰了。美国音乐家联合会刊登报纸广告，抗议用机械播放设备取代现场乐手。1929年刊登在《匹兹堡新闻报》上的一则广告展示了一个标有”罐装音乐/大噪音牌/保证不产生任何智力或情感反应”的罐头图像。

04

Sometimes, live performances incorporate prerecorded sounds. For example, a disc jockey uses disc records for scratching, and some 20th-century works have a solo for an instrument or voice that is performed along with music that is prerecorded onto a tape. Some pop bands use recorded backing tracks. Computers and many keyboards can be programmed to produce and play Musical Instrument Digital Interface (MIDI) music. Audiences can also become performers by participating in karaoke, an activity of Japanese origin centered on a device that plays voice-eliminated versions of well-known songs. Most karaoke machines also have video screens that show lyrics to songs being performed; performers can follow the lyrics as they sing over the instrumental tracks.

有时，现场表演会融合预录的声音。例如，唱片骑师使用黑胶唱片进行搓碟，一些20世纪的作品有一个乐器或人声独奏部分，要与预先录制在磁带上的音乐一起表演。一些流行乐队使用录制的伴奏音轨。计算机和许多键盘乐器可以编程来制作和播放乐器数字接口音乐。观众也可以通过参与卡拉OK成为表演者，这是一种起源于日本的活动，核心是一个播放消除人声的知名歌曲版本的设备。大多数卡拉OK机还配有显示所演唱歌曲歌词的视频屏幕；表演者可以在跟着器乐音轨演唱的同时看歌词。

04

The advent of the Internet and widespread high-speed broadband access has transformed the experience of music, partly through the increased ease of access to recordings of music via streaming video and vastly increased choice of music for consumers. Another effect of the Internet arose with online communities and social media websites like YouTube and Facebook, a social networking service. These sites make it easier for aspiring singers and amateur bands to distribute videos of their songs, connect with other musicians, and gain audience interest. Professional musicians also use YouTube as a free publisher of promotional material. YouTube users, for example, no longer only download and listen to MP3s, but also actively create their own. According to Don Tapscott and Anthony D. Williams, in their book Wikinomics, there has been a shift from a traditional consumer role to what they call a “prosumer” role, a consumer who both creates content and consumes. Manifestations of this in music include the production of mashes, remixes, and music videos by fans.

互联网的出现和高速宽带的广泛普及已经改变了音乐的体验，部分原因在于通过流媒体视频访问音乐录音更加容易，以及消费者对音乐的选择大大增加。互联网的另一个影响体现在在线社区和社交媒体网站，如YouTube和Facebook（一项社交网络服务）。这些网站使有抱负的歌手和业余乐队更容易分发他们歌曲的视频，与其他音乐人联系，并获得听众的兴趣。专业音乐人也使用YouTube作为免费发布宣传材料的平台。例如，YouTube用户不再仅仅下载和收听MP3，还积极地创作他们自己的内容。根据唐·泰普斯科特和安东尼·D·威廉姆斯在他们的《维基经济学》一书中所说，人们的角色已经从传统的消费者转变为所谓的”产消合一者”，即既创造内容又消费内容的消费者。这在音乐中的表现包括粉丝制作的混搭曲、混音版和音乐视频。

04

Music streaming services have further transformed music production. Streaming platforms, such as Spotify and Apple Music, mediate how music is consumed and distributed. This raises questions about how artists market their releases (i.e. as ‘limited edition’), algorithmic suggestions, and affordable access to recorded music.

音乐流媒体服务进一步改变了音乐生产。流媒体平台，如Spotify和Apple Music，调节着音乐的消费和分发方式。这引发了关于艺术家如何营销他们的发行作品（例如作为’限量版’）、算法推荐以及低价获取录制音乐的途径等问题。

04

Generative AI systems, more commonly known as Music Metacreation in music generation, are increasingly being used to create music. A more neutral term is ‘Music Generation Systems’ (MGSs).

生成式AI系统，在音乐生成中更常被称为音乐元创作，正越来越多地被用于创作音乐。一个更中性的术语是”音乐生成系统”。

Education

04

Non-institutional

The incorporation of music into general education from preschool to post secondary education is common in North America and Europe. Involvement in playing and singing music is thought to teach basic skills such as concentration, counting, listening, and cooperation while also promoting understanding of language, improving the ability to recall information, and creating an environment more conducive to learning in other areas. In elementary schools, children often learn to play instruments such as the recorder, sing in small choirs, and learn about the history of Western art music and traditional music. Some elementary school children also learn about popular music styles. In religious schools, children sing hymns and other religious music. In secondary schools (but rarely in primary schools), students may have the opportunity to perform in some types of musical ensembles, such as choirs (a group of singers), marching bands, concert bands, jazz bands, or orchestras. In some school systems, music lessons on how to play instruments may be provided. Some students also take private music lessons after school with a singing teacher or instrument teacher. Amateur musicians typically learn basic musical rudiments (e.g., learning about musical notation for musical scales and rhythms) and beginner- to intermediate-level singing or instrument-playing techniques.

非机构性

将音乐纳入从学前到高等教育的通识教育在北美和欧洲很普遍。人们认为参与演奏和演唱音乐，可以教授基本技能，如专注、计算、倾听和合作，同时也促进对语言的理解，提高回忆信息的能力，并创造一个更有利于其他领域学习的环境。在小学，孩子们经常学习演奏竖笛等乐器，在小合唱团中唱歌，并了解西方艺术音乐和传统音乐的历史。一些小学生也会学习流行音乐风格。在宗教学校，孩子们唱赞美诗和其他宗教音乐。在中学（但在小学较少见），学生可能有机会在某些类型的音乐合奏团中表演，如合唱团（一组歌手）、行进乐队、管乐团、爵士乐队或管弦乐团。在一些学校体系中，可能会提供关于如何演奏乐器的音乐课程。一些学生还会在课后跟随声乐教师或器乐教师上私人音乐课。业余乐手通常会学习基本的音乐基础（例如，学习关于音阶和节奏的音乐记谱法）以及初级到中级水平的演唱或乐器演奏技巧。

04

At the university level, students in most arts and humanities programs can receive credit for taking a few music courses, which typically take the form of an overview course on the history of music, or a music appreciation course that focuses on listening to music and learning about different musical styles. In addition, most North American and European universities have some types of musical ensembles that students in arts and humanities are able to participate in, such as choirs, marching bands, concert bands, or orchestras. The study of Western art music is increasingly common outside of North America and Europe, such as the Indonesian Institute of the Arts in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, or the classical music programs that are available in Asian countries such as South Korea, Japan, and China. At the same time, Western universities and colleges are widening their curriculum to include music of non-Western cultures, such as the music of Africa or Bali (e.g. Gamelan music).

在大学层面，大多数艺术和人文学科课程的学生可以通过修读几门音乐课程获得学分，这些课程通常采取音乐史概论课程，或侧重于聆听音乐和了解不同音乐风格的音乐欣赏课程的形式。此外，大多数北美和欧洲的大学都有某些类型的音乐合奏团，艺术和人文学科的学生能够参与其中，例如合唱团、行进乐队、管乐团或管弦乐团。西方艺术音乐的研究在北美和欧洲以外越来越普遍，例如印度尼西亚日惹的印度尼西亚艺术学院，或韩国、日本和中国等亚洲国家提供的古典音乐课程。与此同时，西方的大学和学院正在拓宽其课程，以包含非西方文化的音乐，例如非洲或巴厘岛的音乐（例如甘美兰音乐）。

04

Institutional

People aiming to become professional musicians, singers, composers, songwriters, music teachers, and practitioners of other music-related professions such as music history professors, sound engineers, and so on study in specialized post-secondary programs offered by colleges, universities, and music conservatories. Some institutions that train individuals for careers in music offer training in a wide range of professions, as is the case with many of the top U.S. universities, which offer degrees in music performance (including singing and playing instruments), music history, music theory, music composition, music education (for individuals aiming to become elementary or high school music teachers), and, in some cases, conducting. On the other hand, some small colleges may only offer training in a single profession (e.g., sound recording).

机构性

立志成为专业音乐家、歌手、作曲家、歌曲作者、音乐教师以及从事其他音乐相关职业（如音乐史教授、音响工程师等）的人，在学院、大学和音乐学院提供的专业高等教育课程中学习。一些培训个人从事音乐职业的机构提供广泛的专业培训，许多美国顶尖大学就是如此，它们提供音乐表演（包括演唱和乐器演奏）、音乐史、音乐理论、音乐作曲、音乐教育（针对有志成为小学或高中音乐教师的人）的学位，在某些情况下还有指挥学位。另一方面，一些小型学院可能只提供单一专业（例如录音）的培训。

04

While most university and conservatory music programs focus on training students in classical music, there are universities and colleges that train musicians for careers as jazz or popular music musicians and composers, with notable U.S. examples including the Manhattan School of Music and the Berklee College of Music. Two schools in Canada which offer professional jazz training are McGill University and Humber College. Individuals aiming at careers in some types of music, such as heavy metal music, country music, or blues are unlikely to become professionals by completing degrees or diplomas. Instead, they typically learn about their style of music by singing or playing in bands (often beginning in amateur bands, cover bands, and tribute bands), studying recordings on DVD and the Internet, and working with already-established professionals in their style of music, either through informal mentoring or regular music lessons. Since the 2000s, the increasing popularity and availability of Internet forums and YouTube “how-to” videos have enabled singers and musicians from metal, blues, and similar genres to improve their skills. Many pop, rock, and country singers train informally with vocal coaches and voice teachers.

Academic study

04

Musicology

Musicology, the academic study of music, is studied in universities and music conservatories. The earliest definitions from the 19th century defined three sub-disciplines of musicology: systematic musicology, historical musicology, and comparative musicology or ethnomusicology. In 2010-era scholarship, one is more likely to encounter a division into music theory, music history, and ethnomusicology. Research in musicology has often been enriched by cross-disciplinary work, for example in the field of psychoacoustics. The study of music of non-Western cultures, and cultural study of music, is called ethnomusicology. Students can pursue study of musicology, ethnomusicology, music history, and music theory through different types of degrees, including bachelor’s, master’s and PhD.

音乐学

音乐学，即对音乐的学术研究，在大学和音乐学院中进行。19世纪最早的定义界定了音乐学的三个分支学科：体系音乐学、历史音乐学和比较音乐学或民族音乐学。在2010年代的学术研究中，更常见的划分是音乐理论、音乐史和民族音乐学。音乐学的研究常常因跨学科工作而丰富，例如在心理声学领域。对非西方文化音乐的研究，以及对音乐的文化研究，称为民族音乐学。学生可以通过不同类型的学位，包括学士、硕士和博士，从事音乐学、民族音乐学、音乐史和音乐理论的研究。

04

Music theory

Music theory is the study of music, generally in a highly technical manner outside of other disciplines. More broadly it refers to any study of music, usually related in some form with compositional concerns, and may include mathematics, physics, and anthropology. What is most commonly taught in beginning music theory classes are guidelines to write in the style of the common practice period, or tonal music. Theory, even of music of the common practice period, may take other forms. Musical set theory is the application of mathematical set theory to music, first applied to atonal music. Speculative music theory, contrasted with analytic music theory, is devoted to the analysis and synthesis of music materials, for example tuning systems, generally as preparation for composition.

音乐理论

音乐理论是对音乐的研究，通常以高度技术性的方式进行，独立于其他学科。更广泛地，它指任何关于音乐的研究，通常以某种形式与作曲问题相关，并可能包括数学、物理学和人类学。初级音乐理论课程中最常教授的是通用实践时期或调性音乐风格的写作指南。理论，即使是关于通用实践时期音乐的理论，也可以采取其他形式。音乐集合论是数学集合论在音乐中的应用，首先应用于无调性音乐。与解析音乐理论相对的推测性音乐理论，致力于音乐材料（例如调律系统）的分析与综合，通常作为作曲的准备。

04

Zoomusicology

Zoomusicology is the study of the music of non-human animals, or the musical aspects of sounds produced by non-human animals. As George Herzog (1941) asked, “do animals have music?” François-Bernard Mâche’s Musique, mythe, nature, ou les Dauphins d’Arion (1983), a study of “ornitho-musicology” using a technique of Nicolas Ruwet’s Language, musique, poésie (1972) paradigmatic segmentation analysis, shows that bird songs are organised according to a repetition-transformation principle. Jean-Jacques Nattiez (1990), argues that “in the last analysis, it is a human being who decides what is and is not musical, even when the sound is not of human origin. If we acknowledge that sound is not organised and conceptualised (that is, made to form music) merely by its producer, but by the mind that perceives it, then music is uniquely human.”

动物音乐学

动物音乐学是对非人类动物的音乐或非人类动物产生的声音中音乐性方面的研究。正如乔治·赫尔佐格（1941）所问，”动物有音乐吗？” 弗朗索瓦-贝尔纳·马什的《音乐、神话、自然，或阿里翁的海豚》（1983）是对”鸟类-音乐学”的研究，运用了尼古拉·鲁韦在《语言、音乐、诗歌》（1972）中的范式切分分析技术，表明鸟鸣是按照重复-转换原则组织的。让-雅克·纳蒂埃（1990）认为，”归根结底，决定什么是音乐、什么不是音乐的是人类，即使声音并非源于人类。如果我们承认，声音并非仅仅由其发出者，而是由感知它的心智来组织和概念化（即形成音乐），那么音乐就是人类所独有的。”

04

Ethnomusicology

In the West, much of the history of music that is taught deals with the Western civilization’s art music, known as classical music. The history of music in non-Western cultures (“world music” or the field of “ethnomusicology”) is also taught in Western universities. This includes the documented classical traditions of Asian countries outside the influence of Western Europe, as well as the folk or indigenous music of various other cultures. Popular or folk styles of music in non-Western countries varied from culture to culture, and period to period. Different cultures emphasised different instruments, techniques, singing styles and uses for music. Music has been used for entertainment, ceremonies, rituals, religious purposes, and for practical and artistic communication. Non-Western music has also been used for propaganda purposes, as was the case with Chinese opera during the Cultural Revolution.

民族音乐学

在西方，所教授的大部分音乐史涉及西方文明的艺术音乐，即所谓的古典音乐。非西方文化的音乐史（”世界音乐”或”民族音乐学”领域）也在西方大学中教授。这包括亚洲国家不受西欧影响的经典传统文献记载，以及各种其他文化的民间或土著音乐。非西方国家的流行或民间音乐风格因文化和时期而异。不同文化强调不同的乐器、技巧、演唱风格和音乐用途。音乐被用于娱乐、典礼、仪式、宗教目的，以及实际和艺术交流。非西方音乐也曾被用于宣传目的，如文化大革命期间的京剧。

04

There is a host of music classifications for non-Western music, many of which are caught up in the argument over the definition of music. Among the largest of these is the division between classical music (or “art” music), and popular music (or commercial music – including non-Western styles of rock, country, and pop music-related styles). Some genres do not fit neatly into one of these “big two” classifications, (such as folk music, world music, or jazz-related music).

非西方音乐有众多分类，其中许多都陷入了关于音乐定义的争论。其中最大的分类之一是古典音乐（或”艺术”音乐）与流行音乐（或商业音乐——包括非西方风格的摇滚、乡村和流行音乐相关风格）之间的划分。有些流派并不能完全归入这两大分类之一（例如民间音乐、世界音乐或爵士相关音乐）。

04

As world cultures have come into greater global contact, their indigenous musical styles have often merged with other styles, which produces new styles. For example, the United States bluegrass style contains elements from Anglo-Irish, Scottish, Irish, German, and African instrumental and vocal traditions, which were able to fuse in the United States’ multi-ethnic “melting pot” society. Some types of world music contain a mixture of non-Western indigenous styles with Western pop music elements. Genres of music are determined as much by tradition and presentation as by the actual music. Some works, like George Gershwin’s Rhapsody in Blue, are claimed by both jazz and classical music, while Gershwin’s Porgy and Bess and Leonard Bernstein’s West Side Story are claimed by both opera and the Broadway musical tradition. Many music festivals for non-Western music include bands and singers from a particular musical genre, such as world music.

随着世界文化进入更广泛的全球接触，它们的本土音乐风格经常与其他风格融合，从而产生新的风格。例如，美国的蓝草音乐风格包含来自盎格鲁-爱尔兰、苏格兰、爱尔兰、德国以及非洲器乐和声乐传统的元素，这些元素得以在美国的多族裔”大熔炉”社会中融合。某些类型的世界音乐包含了非西方本土风格与西方流行音乐元素的混合。音乐流派既取决于传统和呈现方式，也取决于实际音乐本身。有些作品，如乔治·格什温的《蓝色狂想曲》，同时被爵士乐和古典音乐声称属于己方，而格什温的《波吉与贝丝》和伦纳德·伯恩斯坦的《西区故事》，则同时被歌剧和百老汇音乐剧传统所声称。许多非西方音乐的音乐节包括来自特定音乐流派的乐队和歌手，例如世界音乐。

04

Indian music, for example, is one of the oldest and longest living types of music, and is still widely heard and performed in South Asia, as well as internationally (especially since the 1960s). Indian music has mainly three forms of classical music, Hindustani, Carnatic, and Dhrupad styles. It has also a large repertoire of styles, which involve only percussion music such as the talavadya performances famous in South India.

Therapy

04

Music therapy is an interpersonal process in which a trained therapist uses music and all of its facets—physical, emotional, mental, social, aesthetic, and spiritual—to help clients to improve or maintain their health. In some instances, the client’s needs are addressed directly through music; in others they are addressed through the relationships that develop between the client and therapist. Music therapy is used with individuals of all ages and with a variety of conditions, including: psychiatric disorders, medical problems, physical disabilities, sensory impairments, developmental disabilities, substance abuse issues, communication disorders, interpersonal problems, and aging. It is also used to improve learning, build self-esteem, reduce stress, support physical exercise, and facilitate a host of other health-related activities. Music therapists may encourage clients to sing, play instruments, create songs, or do other musical activities.

音乐治疗是一个人际互动的过程，训练有素的治疗师利用音乐及其所有层面——身体的、情感的、心理的、社会的、审美的和精神的——来帮助来访者改善或维持他们的健康。在某些情况下，来访者的需求直接通过音乐来解决；在其他情况下，则通过来访者与治疗师之间发展的关系来解决。音乐治疗用于各个年龄段和各种状况的个体，包括：精神障碍、医疗问题、身体残疾、感觉障碍、发育障碍、药物滥用问题、沟通障碍、人际问题以及衰老。它也用于改善学习、建立自尊、减轻压力、支持体育锻炼，并促进许多其他与健康相关的活动。音乐治疗师可能会鼓励来访者唱歌、演奏乐器、创作歌曲或进行其他音乐活动。
